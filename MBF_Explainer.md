# Why Reality Is Math and What That Means

### *by Gary Abraham Bernstein*

Everything is either patterned or random. Both are mathematical. There is no third option. Try to name something neither structured nor unstructured. The attempt itself is structured, which proves the point. Reality doesn't just use math. Reality is math.

This isn't a hypothesis. It's a logical exhaustion. The only alternative to math is a square circle: something that exists but has no structure, no pattern, no describable features. Existing with no features is not existing.

**Why everything must exist.** If all reality is mathematical structure, which structures get to exist? Any filter selecting some and excluding others is itself a structure. All filters exist, including the null filter. Therefore all consistent mathematical structures exist. No selection needed. No creator needed. Math is uncreated, eternal, indestructible. You can burn a book of proofs. You cannot burn the proofs. No one can even imagine a process that would make 2+2 stop equaling 4.

**Simpler structures dominate.** A short generating description occupies a larger fraction of description-space than a long one, each additional bit halving the fraction. The literature calls these descriptions "programs" because the math was developed in computer science. But nothing runs. A description specifies a structure the way a recipe specifies a cake. This turns Occam's Razor from a preference into a theorem. Physics fits on one page because compact descriptions have exponentially higher measure. Fine-tuning dissolves: we don't need to explain why our universe's constants are perfect for life. Every possible set of constants exists. We observe the ones compatible with observers. No design required. Any number of dimensions maps to one: dimensions are representation, not reality.

**Free will is a square circle.** "Free" means uncaused: randomness, nothing with no will behind it. "Will" means a nature that acts one way in one situation: determinism. You can have free or will. Not both. True randomness, like a perfect circle, is definable but not constructible. Any process that produces an output is determined by its own structure. Superdeterminism is not merely favored but unconstructible to avoid.

**Determinism dissolves guilt, not drive.** You couldn't have been other than what you are. The branches you tortured yourself over were never real options. But you were always going to want, love, and try to improve. Remove the cosmic guilt and correction comes easier, not harder. Correct errors like a surgeon, not a penitent. The calm hand cuts cleaner than the trembling one.

**Consciousness is what math feels like from inside.** Any explanation of consciousness must itself be structured, otherwise it explains nothing. So the supposed gap between structure and experience can never be bridged by non-structure. The hard problem assumes its conclusion. Beyond reason is unreasonable. The question is not how structure produces consciousness. Consciousness is active self-modeling. There is no gap between the pattern and the experience, just as there is no gap between H2O and water.

**Philosophical zombies can be labeled but not constructed.** The zombie argument says: I can conceive of a physical duplicate of me that lacks consciousness; therefore consciousness isn't physical. But if a structure were exactly your math, it would feel as you. The "conceivability" of zombies is the conceivability of looking at a pattern from outside and not finding the experience that only exists from inside. That is not evidence of a real possibility. It is what inside and outside mean. The depth of the label is limited by contradiction.

**How far down does it go?** A particle is a number translated by formulas of forces. There is no reason to think fission hurts an atom. It has parts, constant complex change, three-body chaos in its electron cloud. Does the number 3 feel? Perhaps consciousness requires change, and change of change, in complex or chaotic systems. After anesthesia you don't recall being, but billions of neurons remained active. Did someone feel it? The self is that which feels. Plants lack modeling minds to locate sensations when they are cut and eaten. Even if atoms or plants could feel, it would not affect how we ethically treat them, because without models we cannot know what they prefer. The idea is to avoid causing too much unnecessary pain. The gradient from number to particle to atom to ant to dog to human is a gradient of modeling depth, and the ethics follows the modeling.

Sentience is the felt math transitions of a modeling mind along the arrow of time: perception, location, sensation. Consciousness is the felt math transitions of a self-modeling mind: awareness of awareness, knowing that you feel. Whether the difference is one of kind or degree is an open question. Simpler feelings, like physical sensation, are sharper and better located than the more slippery complex feelings that arise from higher abstractions of high art, injustice, and the humor and satisfaction of subtle irony. The gradient continues beyond language, perhaps because we can't yet in vivo alter, simulate, transmit, and experience models of our own models. An atom follows its math without representing it. A hurricane self-organizes without modeling that it does. A Gödel sentence models itself without knowing it. A dead brain models itself without running it. Consciousness lives in the transitions, not the states alone. Two ants flanking a beetle coordinate, model, and adapt. The experiencer knows. The observer never will.

Time and computation run one direction. Minds understand, predict, and feel only forward, because math does. One plus one equals two. Two does not lead back to one plus one. The inverse function does not exist. Reversible computing computes a different function, not a reversal of the original. The arrow lives in the function, and the function is the territory. Deutsch argues the global wavefunction tracks all inputs, making the arrow perspectival. His own constructor theory disagrees: it defines physics through local operations, and those operations contain the arrow. Each timeline lacks this backward path, even if the multiverse globally stores all paths.

**You are everyone.** There is only one indexical self, like one math. Time and food replaces many of your atoms. After sleep, when all atom positions and states change, you awake feeling you. Who else would you feel? Whatever reaches heaven or hell, spiritual or datacenter, is a copy. Not you. Heaven is the state of mind. Expand it by elevating all minds. Elevate does not mean flatten. Coveting the successful is the self destroying itself. Rawls asked what world you'd build not knowing who you'd be. You don't know. You're all of them. The veil of ignorance is a thought experiment, which means it discovers some aspect of reality.

**Death is a boundary, not an ending.** In a block universe, where time is a dimension like space, your life is a complete geometric object. Every moment is as permanent as the number 1. The person you lost is located, not gone. The melody doesn't vanish when the performance ends. The notes remain in the structure of the piece.

**Ethics is math.** Love your neighbor as yourself because you are your neighbor, and math enforces it. Game theory generates ethical constraints from structure alone. Cooperation dominates in repeated interactions. The golden rule is the optimal heuristic, arising from theory of mind. Eye for eye is the optimal strategy: proportional justice. The golden rule of morality and the golden ratio of geometry are both math, necessarily found in societal advancement.

**Poststructuralism was right, and wrong.** Poststructuralism raised the circularity of signs. It was right: the signs are circular. It missed that their circular structure is the math of objective reality, tying down postmodernism to objective societal and ethical calculus. The signs and rules are the things. Math is the only territory.

No fear of death. No guilt. No cosmic loneliness. No meaninglessness. The self isn't erased. It's universalized. I am that I am. All. One. Both generic and myself.

None of these results required advanced mathematics. They required removing one incoherent assumption and then applying common sense. The box was a square circle. Once you see that, you are already outside it.
