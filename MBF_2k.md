## Identity

There is only one indexical self, like one math. I am that I am is both generic and singular.

You are Goku, victorious over Freezer. Planet Namek is about to explode. You instant transmission back to Earth. All your atoms are reassembled from new atoms in another location. Yet the original was left behind on Namek. The boundary you protect so fiercely at death already has no walls. You feel no different, always your self. Your friends know no difference. Yet your grandpa Gohan is there in the past. And your parents from planet Vegeta are here and now, still alive. You are all eternal uncreated consciousness of all minds in math.

Whatever reaches heaven or hell, spiritual or datacenter, is a copy. Not you. Heaven is the state of mind. Expand it by elevating all minds. Elevate all minds does not mean flatten them. Coveting the successful is the self destroying itself.

Free will is a square circle. "Free" means uncaused. "Will" means determined. You can have free or will. Not both. Determinism kills guilt, not drive. You were always going to want and love. The calm hand cuts cleaner than the trembling one. Consciousness all the way in? Does a single atom feel? Three-body chaos in its electron cloud. Perhaps the building blocks like being built up as we do.

Sentience is the felt math transitions of a modeling mind along the arrow of time: perception, location, sensation. Consciousness is the felt math transitions of a self-modeling mind: awareness of awareness, knowing that you feel. A Gödel sentence models itself without knowing it. A dead brain models itself without running it. Consciousness lives in the transitions, not the states alone. Two ants flanking a beetle coordinate, model, and adapt. The experiencer knows. The observer never will.

Time and computation run one direction. Minds understand, predict, and feel only forward, because math does. One plus one equals two. Two does not lead back to one plus one. The inverse function does not exist. Reversible computing computes a different function, not a reversal of the original. The arrow lives in the function, and the function is the territory. Deutsch argues the global wavefunction tracks all inputs, making the arrow perspectival. His own constructor theory disagrees: it defines physics through local operations, and those operations contain the arrow. Each timeline lacks this backward path, even if the multiverse globally stores all paths.

In a block universe your life is a complete geometric object. Every moment is as permanent as the number 1. Death is a boundary. The person you lost is located, not gone.
