A38 symbolic string counts

The earlier raw and compressed totals were approximate storage counts of human readable formula strings. They were not executable complete physics. Unicode encoding, delimiters and compression overhead must be specified even for that limited storage task.

Adding an extracted binary count to a symbolic summary does not establish a parameter budget or the total complexity of physics. The prior claim that 9,216 bits simultaneously represented parameter precision and all known physics is withdrawn.

Use A38_C_BINARY_BIT_COUNT.md for the implementation audit and A38_Kolmogorov_Standard_Model.md for the scoped comparison proposal. No new universal bit count is reported here.
