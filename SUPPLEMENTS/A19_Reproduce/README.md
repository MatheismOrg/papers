# A19 data package

Rebuilds the results of "Dark Matter Behaves Like Matter" from public data with independent code, and maps every number in the paper to the original code that produced it.

This package covers protocols 1 to 9 and Sections 2 to 5.3 of the paper. The one formula of Section 6, with protocols 10 to 21, the ten forecasts and their saved results, is in `one_formula/`, which has its own README. The paper and its Supplemental Notes are in this repository's `PDF/`, `SOURCE_MD/` and `SOURCE_HTML/` folders, as `A19_Dark_Matter_Behaves_Like_Matter` and `A19_Dark_Matter_Behaves_Like_Matter_Supplemental_Notes`.

Section numbers in the number map follow the paper's version of 11 November 2026, the version this package was last checked against. In it, Sections 1 to 5.3 keep their earlier numbers, the one formula is Section 6, and the section on circular cluster benchmarks is Section 7 (it was Section 6 in the version of 31 October 2026). Table 1 now lists the protocols, the disk table is Table 2 (it was Table 1), the table of dwarfs no rule had scored is Table S1 of the Supplemental Notes (it was Table 2), and Tables 3 to 6 keep their numbers.

## Protocol 3 (session SA73, 31 October 2026)

PROTOCOL_v3.txt was written and hashed (SHA-256 eafbb036..., 00:18:25 US Pacific) before any prediction was computed for its dwarfs: those no earlier test scored, with measured dispersions (Set M) or upper limits (Set U), and those with no dispersion (Set F, forecasts). Before writing it, the database was confirmed to be at its latest commit and the candidates' names, hosts, distances and published upper limits were listed. A first save of the same protocol at 00:18:17 carried a mistyped writing time (00:25); it was corrected and re-hashed at 00:18:25, before any scoring. The forecast file A19_Forecasts_v3.csv was hashed at 00:22:53 (A19_Forecasts_v3.sha256). Neither primary criterion was met: on Set M the stellar rule has the lowest error of seven models but five dwarfs give a bootstrap interval spanning zero; on Set U it exceeds two upper limits where MOND's relation exceeds none. The subhalo lead (E1) exceeds three and fails its test.

Four forecast rows (dw1008p7038b, dw1009p7032, dw1242p4115, dw1250p5056) carry database half-light radii of 9 to 33 kpc, far above other dwarfs of their luminosity; their forecasts are as computed from the database and should be read with that in mind. None is in the paper's table.

## Protocol 4 (session SA73, turn 2, 31 October 2026)

PROTOCOL_v4.txt was written and hashed (SHA-256 6e7b4d87..., 00:46:30 US Pacific) before any likelihood was computed. It scores all 85 dwarfs scored so far in one likelihood, every value from the Local Volume Database: measured dispersions as values with their errors, upper limits as ceilings, each model's only free number its scatter. Its primary criterion was met: the stellar rule beats the relation with the disk a0 by 36.2 log-likelihood units, bootstrap 23.6 to 48.3. It also writes 68 percent ranges for protocol 3's forecasts (A19_Forecast_Ranges_v4.csv), whose central values are unchanged.
