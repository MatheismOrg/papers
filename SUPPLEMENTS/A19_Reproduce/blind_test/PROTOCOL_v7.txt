A19 CLUSTER HALO MASSES AGAINST PUBLISHED LENSING MASSES
PROTOCOL VERSION 7
Written 31 October 2026, 02:10 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5), session SA73, turn 5, for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

PURPOSE
Protocol 6, check D, compared the Moster halo masses with NFW masses that this work fitted to the CLASH
accelerations of Tian et al. (2020). An outside review (Perplexity, 31 October) noted that this is an internal
consistency check, not a comparison with published lensing masses. Tian et al. computed those accelerations from
the spherical NFW fits of Umetsu et al. (2016), whose Table 2 gives M_200c and c_200c for all 20 clusters. This
protocol compares the halo masses of protocols 5 and 6 with those published masses, by selection route, and is
fixed before any of the comparisons is computed.

WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN
All results of protocols 5 and 6, including check D's fitted masses (A19_Cluster_Audit_v6_Hosts.csv).
Umetsu et al. (2016), Table 2, read on 31 October 2026 and transcribed to data/Clusters/umetsu2016_table2.csv
(two separate extractions of the arXiv text agree on every row). By eye its masses lie within about 0.05 dex of
check D's fitted masses, so the outcome below is largely foreseen. Umetsu et al. adopt h = 0.7 and
Omega_m = 0.27 and give M_200c in 10^14 M_sun h70^-1; they group the 20 clusters as 16 chosen by X-ray criteria
(temperature above 5 keV, smooth X-ray morphology) and 4 chosen for high lensing magnification. Tian et al.
(2020) computed the CLASH accelerations from Umetsu et al.'s NFW posteriors under that cosmology, and take the
BCG stellar masses of Cooke et al. (2016) as total masses.

DATA
data/Clusters/umetsu2016_table2.csv, and blind_test/A19_Cluster_Audit_v6_Hosts.csv for protocol 5's halo masses
(log_Mh_deterministic), protocol 6's posterior medians (log_Mh_posterior_median) and check D's fitted masses
(log_M200_lensing).

METHOD
(1) Published masses as given (physical suns for h = 0.7), the convention in which this work uses Tian et al.'s
    accelerations; as a check, scaled by 0.7/0.671 for h = 0.671, since lensing masses scale as 1/h.
(2) For each cluster, r = log10(M_Moster / M_Umetsu) for the mean inversion and for the posterior median, and
    the scatter expected from the published error alone, s = e_M / (M ln 10).
(3) Reported for all 20 clusters and for each route (16 X-ray, 4 high magnification): the median and standard
    deviation of r, the root mean square of s, and the number of clusters with |r| at most 2 s.
(4) Check D against the published masses: median and largest |log10(M_checkD / M_Umetsu)|.

OUTCOMES
The halo masses are called consistent with the published lensing masses only if, for the mean inversion or for
the probabilistic one, the median r lies within 0.2 dex of zero and the standard deviation of r is at most twice
the root mean square of s. Check D is called a faithful stand-in for the published masses if its largest
difference is below 0.1 dex.

REPORTING
All outcomes are reported whatever they show. Nothing changes after this file's SHA-256 is recorded.
