A19 BLIND DWARF TEST: PROTOCOL, FIXED BEFORE ANY NEW DWARF IS SCORED
Written 30 October 2026, 22:55 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5) for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

PURPOSE
The stellar halo rule was proposed after the baryonic rule's transfer to the original 44 dwarf
spheroidals had been seen. This test applies the rules, frozen on the 149 SPARC disks, to dwarf
spheroidals that were not among those 44 and have not been scored by any rule in this project.

FROZEN INPUTS (no refitting of any kind)
- Rules: frozen_disk_rules.json (A19_Disk_Fit_SPARC.py, all 149 disks, each galaxy weighted equally):
  stellar rule A, B, C; baryonic rule A, B, C; radial acceleration relation a0 (disk fit).
  Published a0 = 1.20e-10 m/s^2 and the published Moster, Naab and White (2013) relation are controls.
- Data: Local Volume Database (Pace 2025), commit c9fb49c620adab3df32c7d206e8599f4bb624a3a,
  data/dwarf_all.csv, sha256 c5c029dce9970e7b70fc329cc46dbf2ed02b308901579a3f2aa756887d542b26.

SELECTION (applied in this order; every exclusion is listed with its reason)
1. Not one of the original 44 (the LVDB keys listed in A19_Dwarfs_LVDB.py, including Segue 2 and Andromeda XII).
2. confirmed_real = 1 and confirmed_galaxy = 1.
3. Host is the Milky Way, the LMC, the SMC or M31 (host in mw, lmc, smc, m_031), as for the original sample.
4. M_V fainter than -14 (excludes compact and dwarf ellipticals such as M32, NGC 147, NGC 185, NGC 205).
5. A measured line-of-sight dispersion: vlos_sigma present and no upper limit (vlos_sigma_ul empty).
6. M_V, rhalf_physical and rhalf_sph_physical present.
7. Measured HI mass below a tenth of the stellar mass (2 L_V); an HI upper limit passes.
8. Not a known tidally disrupting system, named in advance: Sagittarius I, Tucana III, Bootes III.

METHOD (as A19 Section 2.1)
One point per dwarf at r = (4/3) R_e, R_e the circularized half-light radius (rhalf_sph_physical);
g_obs = 3 sigma^2 / r; stellar mass 2 L_V with L_V from M_V (solar M_V = 4.83); g_bar = G (M_star/2) / r^2;
halo predictions as in A19 Section 3.1 with M_p = M_star (no gas).

PRIMARY OUTCOME
Root-mean-square error in log10 g (dex) of the stellar rule against the radial acceleration relation with
its disk a0. Success: stellar RMSE lower, and the 95 percent paired bootstrap interval of the RMSE
difference (20,000 resamples of whole dwarfs, seed 20261030) below zero.
SECONDARY OUTCOMES
Stellar rule against the baryonic rule (same criterion); against Moster; against the published a0.
Counts of dwarfs closer under the stellar rule.
SENSITIVITIES (reported, not used for the verdict)
Major-axis radii; mass-to-light ratios 1 and 3; luminosity at or above 1e5 and below.

REPORTING
All outcomes are reported whatever they show. No rule, coefficient, selection step or metric changes
after this file's SHA-256 is recorded.
