A19 TEST ON DWARFS NO RULE HAS SCORED, AND FORECASTS FOR DWARFS NOT YET MEASURED
PROTOCOL VERSION 3
Written 31 October 2026, 00:18 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5), session SA73, for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

PURPOSE
Protocols 1 and 2 tested the frozen rules on dwarf spheroidals of the Milky Way and Andromeda systems with
measured dispersions. Two kinds of data in the same database were never scored: dwarfs outside those
systems, and dwarfs whose dispersion is only an upper limit. Leaving out upper limits drops the dwarfs that
move slowest, which favors rules that predict fast motion, so they are scored here. The protocol also fixes
forecasts for dwarfs whose dispersion has not been measured, to be checked when it is.

WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN
The frozen rules had been scored on: the original 44 (original44_keys.txt); the 42 of the Local Volume
Database check (the 44 keys in A19_Dwarfs_LVDB.py less Segue 2 and Andromeda XII, which that check dropped
for upper-limit dispersions); the 27 of protocol 1; and the 25 of protocol 2. Before this file was written,
the database was checked to be at its latest commit (c9fb49c is the head of main on 31 October 2026), and
the names, hosts, distances and published upper-limit values of the candidate dwarfs were listed. No
prediction of any rule has been computed for any dwarf in the sets below.

DATA
Local Volume Database (Pace 2025), commit c9fb49c620adab3df32c7d206e8599f4bb624a3a, data/dwarf_all.csv,
sha256 c5c029dce9970e7b70fc329cc46dbf2ed02b308901579a3f2aa756887d542b26. In the database's convention an
upper limit is at 95 percent confidence (some sources give 90 or 84 percent).

FROZEN RULES (no refitting of any kind)
As protocols 1 and 2: frozen_disk_rules.json (stellar rule, baryonic rule, radial acceleration relation with
the disk a0); the published a0 = 1.20e-10 m/s^2; the published Moster, Naab and White (2013) relation.
Two environment variants, published constants only, as in A19_Environment_Tests.py:
(E1) Stellar rule with the subhalo concentration factor 1 - 0.54 log10(D/R_200,host) (Moline et al. 2017),
     host M_200 = 1.0e12 suns for the Milky Way (satellites of the LMC and SMC use their Galactocentric
     distance) and 1.5e12 for M31; factor 1 for a dwarf with no host, in another group, or beyond the
     host's R_200.
(E2) MOND with the external field as McGaugh and Milgrom (2013): sigma = min(sigma_iso, sigma_efe),
     sigma_iso = (4/81 G M a0)^(1/4), sigma_efe = [G M a0 / (3 r g_ext)]^(1/2), a0 = 1.21e-10 m/s^2,
     M = 2 L_V, r = (4/3) R_e, g_ext = V^2/D with V = 200 km/s for the Milky Way (LMC and SMC satellites
     at their Galactocentric distance) and 230 km/s for M31; for a dwarf with no host, the larger of the two
     giants' fields; not computed for dwarfs in other groups.

SELECTION (common steps, in this order; every exclusion is listed with its reason)
1. Not scored before: not in any of the four lists above.
2. confirmed_real = 1 and confirmed_galaxy = 1.
3. M_V present and fainter than -14.
4. rhalf_physical and rhalf_sph_physical present.
5. Measured HI below a tenth of the stellar mass (2 L_V); an HI upper limit passes.
6. Not a named tidally disrupting system or compact non-dwarf: Sagittarius I, Tucana III, Bootes III
   (as protocols 1 and 2) and SUCD1 (an ultracompact dwarf of the Sombrero galaxy).
Then, by the dispersion:
Set M (measured): vlos_sigma present and vlos_sigma_ul empty. Any host: satellites of the Milky Way, LMC,
      SMC or M31; dwarfs with no host; dwarfs in other groups.
Set U (upper limits): vlos_sigma_ul present. Any host.
Set F (forecasts): neither present. Any host.

METHOD (as A19 Section 2.1)
One point per dwarf at r = (4/3) R_e, R_e the circularized half-light radius (rhalf_sph_physical);
g_obs = 3 sigma^2 / r; stellar mass 2 L_V, L_V from M_V with solar M_V = 4.83; g_bar = G (M_star/2) / r^2;
halo predictions as A19 Section 3.1 with M_p = M_star. A model's predicted dispersion is
sigma_pred = (g_pred r / 3)^(1/2).

OUTCOMES
Set M, primary: root-mean-square error in log10 g (dex) of the stellar rule against the radial acceleration
relation with the disk a0. Success: stellar RMSE lower, and the 95 percent paired bootstrap interval of the
RMSE difference (20,000 resamples of whole dwarfs, seed 20261031) below zero.
Set M, secondary: the same comparison against the baryonic rule, Moster, the published a0, (E1) and (E2)
where computed; counts of dwarfs closer under the stellar rule; every dwarf's residual listed.
Set U, primary: for each model, the number of upper limits its central predicted dispersion exceeds.
Success for the stellar rule: no more exceedances than the radial acceleration relation with the disk a0.
Set U, secondary: the number exceeded by more than a factor of 1.46 in dispersion (the stellar rule's
intrinsic scatter of 0.33 dex in acceleration on the 25 dwarfs of protocol 2, halved for dispersion), with
the same tolerance for every model; the median of log10(sigma_pred / sigma_ul) for each model; the (E1) lead
passes if it exceeds no more upper limits than the stellar rule without it.
Set F: for every dwarf, sigma_pred for the stellar rule, (E1), Moster, the relation with the disk and the
published a0, and (E2) where computed; and the ratio of the stellar rule's forecast to (E2)'s, or to the
published-a0 relation's where (E2) is not computed. A forecast counts as decisive where the two differ by
more than a factor of 2. The forecasts are written to A19_Forecasts_v3.csv, whose SHA-256 is recorded
before release. The paper's table shows the satellites of the Milky Way, LMC, SMC and M31 and the dwarfs
with no host within 1.5 Mpc of the Milky Way; the file holds all.

SENSITIVITIES (reported, not used for the verdict)
Major-axis radii; mass-to-light ratios 1 and 3; Milky Way speeds of 175 and 220 km/s for (E2).

REPORTING
All outcomes are reported whatever they show, in the paper and the supplement. No rule, coefficient,
selection step or metric changes after this file's SHA-256 is recorded.
