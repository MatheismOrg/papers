A19 HALO RULE AGAINST THE KiDS-1000 LENSING RADIAL ACCELERATION RELATION OF ISOLATED GALAXIES
PROTOCOL VERSION 9
Written 31 October 2026, US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5), session SA73, turn 9, for Gary Abraham Bernstein.
The clock time is recorded with the SHA-256 in PROTOCOL_v9.sha256.

PURPOSE
Brouwer et al. (2021, A&A 650, A113; B21) measured the lensing radial acceleration relation (the excess surface
density, ESD, stacked in 15 bins of baryonic acceleration g_bar) of 259,383 isolated KiDS-bright galaxies at
0.1 < z < 0.5. They split the lenses into four stellar-mass bins, and into early and late types at matched stellar
mass, finding early types 0.17 dex (split by Sersic index) to 0.27 dex (split by u-r colour) above late types.
The stellar rule, frozen on rotation curves, gives a smaller galaxy of the same stellar mass a heavier halo. Early
types are smaller than disks of the same mass, so the rule predicts part of the split with no new parameter. This
protocol forward-models the frozen rule into the same stacked profiles and asks (A) how much of each split the
rule's size term accounts for, and (B) whether the rule matches the absolute lensing profiles inside 0.3 Mpc,
above all for late-type galaxies, the kind it was calibrated on.

METHOD
(1) Lenses. Stellar masses (h70^-2 suns, Chabrier, as published) from the digitized Figure 7, in 0.05 dex steps:
    the matched distribution min(red, blue) for both Figure 8 splits (B21 show it for the colour split only); the
    red and blue counts from 8.5 to 11.0 for the four mass bins and the full isolated sample, which also set the
    type mix at each mass (by colour). All lenses at z = 0.25, B21's mean.
(2) Binning, as B21: galaxy mass M_gal = M*(1 + f_cold), log f_cold = -0.69 log M* + 6.63 (Boselli et al. 2014),
    for every lens of either type. The g_bar bins are 15 logarithmic bins between 1e-15 and 5e-12 m s^-2; a lens
    contributes to bin [g1, g2] the annulus sqrt(G M_gal/g2) < R < sqrt(G M_gal/g1). Physical units with h = 0.7.
(3) The frozen stellar rule, A, B and C from blind_test/frozen_disk_rules.json, M* taken as published.
    Late types (blue, or n < 2): R_e and M_HI from the fits to the 149 SPARC disks used in protocol 8,
    log R_e = -1.6721 + 0.2245 log M* and log M_HI = 4.1156 + 0.5396 log M*, with M_p = M* + 1.33 M_HI.
    Early types (red, or n > 2): M_p = M*, and R_e = (disk R_e) x 10^D(M*), where D is the difference between the
    median log sizes of early- and late-type galaxies at z = 0.25 in van der Wel et al. (2014, Table 2):
    -0.22, -0.33, -0.28 and -0.16 dex at log M* = 9.25, 9.75, 10.25 and 10.75, interpolated linearly in log M*
    and held constant beyond the ends.
    M_ref = M_p/f_b + 10^A (M*/10^10)^B (R_e/3 kpc)^C is M_200c in suns relative to the critical density at
    z = 0.25 in the rule's cosmology (H0 = 67.1, Omega_m = 0.3175), with c = 10^0.905 (M_ref h/10^12)^-0.101,
    h = 0.671; an untruncated NFW profile.
(4) One lens's model ESD: Delta Sigma(R) = Delta Sigma_NFW(R) (Wright and Brainerd 2000) + M_p/(pi R^2), averaged
    over each bin's annulus with weight proportional to area. A sample's stacked model: the mean over its lenses
    weighted by count times M_gal (at fixed redshift the number of lens-source pairs in a bin's annulus is
    proportional to M_gal).
(5) Inner bins: a bin is inner when its whole annulus lies inside R = 0.3 Mpc (B21's limit, beyond which false
    isolated lenses may add signal) for a lens of the sample's mean galaxy mass, that is g1 >= G <M_gal>/(0.3 Mpc)^2,
    with <M_gal> the count-weighted mean from step (1) for the Figure 8 samples and the full sample, and B21's
    stated means for the four mass bins.
(6) The model profiles of every sample, and of every sensitivity case, are computed and saved, with their
    SHA-256 and clock time, before any profile or covariance value is read.

OUTCOMES
(A) The split. S = log10 of the mean over bins of ESD_early/ESD_late, for the data and for the model; over the inner
    bins (primary) and over all 15 bins (B21's definition). sigma_S from 20,000 draws of both profiles from the
    released covariance. With T = max(2 sigma_S, 0.25 S_data), for each split separately: the rule ACCOUNTS FOR
    the split if |S_model - S_data| <= T; OVERPREDICTS it if S_model > S_data + T; ACCOUNTS FOR PART if
    0.25 S_data <= S_model < S_data - T; DOES NOT if S_model < 0.25 S_data.
(B) Amplitudes. For each sample (blue, red, n < 2, n > 2, the four mass bins, the full isolated sample), the
    generalized least-squares amplitude a that best scales the model to the data over the inner bins with the
    released covariance, with its error. The rule is CONSISTENT with a sample if |log10 a| <= 0.1 dex, or within
    twice its error where that exceeds 0.05 dex. Judged for the late types (blue and n < 2), the rule's home
    ground; reported for every sample with the same test. The chi2 of the unscaled model, inner and all bins, is
    reported.
SENSITIVITY (reported, not judged): early types at half the disk size (protocol 8's bracket) and at the disk
size; van der Wel's medians as the sizes of both types; SPARC gas for early types; z = 0.15 and 0.35; the rule's
M* shifted by +0.1 and -0.1 dex; Dutton and Maccio concentrations at z = 0.25; 0.15 dex lognormal halo scatter at
fixed stellar mass (mean ESD over the scatter); the stellar-mass distributions shifted by +0.1 and -0.1 dex.
Known limits, stated now: one redshift for all lenses; no two-halo term; no satellites or miscentring; stellar-
mass errors and Eddington bias ignored; the matched distribution of the colour split used for the Sersic split.
