A19 AUDIT OF THE CLUSTER-SCALE TEST (PROTOCOL 5)
PROTOCOL VERSION 6
Written 31 October 2026, 01:23 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5), session SA73, turn 4, for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

PURPOSE
An outside review (Perplexity, 31 October) accepted protocol 5's acceleration scores but asked whether they
survive four checks: a deterministic inversion of the mean Moster relation ignores its published scatter;
five CLASH halos came out above 10^16 suns, two at the search bound; the halo masses were never compared
with independent cluster masses; and stellar masses beyond the range the relation was fitted on were used.
This protocol fixes those checks before any of them is computed.

WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN
All of protocol 5's results (A19_Clusters_v5_Results.json and _Points.csv). Moster et al. (2013) define
halo mass at 200 times the critical density ("All virial masses are computed with respect to 200 times the
critical density"), so the M_200c concentration of Dutton and Maccio (2014) used in protocol 5 matches it;
they adopt 0.15 dex lognormal scatter in stellar mass at fixed halo mass; their local stellar mass function
reaches about 10^11.7 suns; their parameters assume h = 0.704 with masses in suns.

DATA AND MODELS
As protocol 5 (data/Clusters/, frozen_disk_rules.json, the radial acceleration relation with the disk a0
as the comparison), with these checks:

(A) Root status. For each host, whether the bounded search found a root (|log M*_model - log M*_obs| below
    0.001 dex) or stopped at the bound 10^16.5.
(B) Supported range. Protocol 5's deterministic prediction scored only on hosts whose BCG stellar mass is
    at most 10^11.7 suns, beside the full sample.
(C) Probabilistic inversion. For each host, the posterior over log M_h from 9 to 16.5 (step 0.005) is
    p(log M_h | M*_obs, z) proportional to N(log M*_obs; log M*_Moster(M_h, z), s) x dn/dln M_h (z),
    with s = (0.15^2 + e^2)^(1/2), e the published log M* error (MaNGA) or 0.10 dex (CLASH, none published),
    and dn/dln M_h the Tinker et al. (2008) mass function for M_200c at the host's redshift (colossus,
    cosmology H0 = 67.1, Omega_m = 0.3175, Omega_b = 0.049, sigma_8 = 0.8344, n_s = 0.9624). The predicted
    log g at each point is the posterior mean of log10[g_bar + g_NFW(r; M_h, z)], with protocol 5's halo.
    Selection is not modeled: CLASH was chosen among the most massive clusters, so this prior pulls their
    halo masses low, while the deterministic inversion runs high; the two bracket the effect of scatter.
    Scored as protocol 5 (hosts weighted equally, 5,000 resamples of hosts, seed 20261031) against the
    relation with the disk a0.
(D) Independent masses. For each CLASH cluster, an NFW M_200c and concentration fitted to its published
    accelerations at 100 to 600 kpc (total mass, g = G M(<r) / r^2, least squares in log g, c bounded 1 to
    20, at the cluster's redshift), compared with the deterministic and the probabilistic Moster halo
    masses: median and scatter of log10(M_Moster / M_lensing). No independent masses exist here for the
    MaNGA galaxies.
(E) Cosmology. Protocol 5's deterministic prediction rerun with h = 0.704 in rho_crit and the
    concentration's mass pivot, as Moster's parameters assume.

OUTCOMES
All checks are reported. The acceleration result of protocol 5 is called robust to scatter only if the
probabilistic inversion (C) also has a lower score than the relation with the disk a0, with the 95 percent
bootstrap interval of the difference below zero. The halo masses are called consistent with lensing only
if the median log ratio in (D) lies within 0.2 dex of zero for the deterministic or the probabilistic
masses.

REPORTING
All outcomes are reported whatever they show. Nothing changes after this file's SHA-256 is recorded.
