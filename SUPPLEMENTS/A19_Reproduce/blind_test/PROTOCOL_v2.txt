A19 OUT-OF-SAMPLE DWARF TEST, PROTOCOL VERSION 2 (CORRECTION OF VERSION 1)
Written 30 October 2026, 23:46 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5) for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

WHY A SECOND VERSION
Version 1 (PROTOCOL.txt, SHA-256 4465bfca...) excluded "the original 44" using the list of 44 dwarfs in a
table supplied by another AI (Argon), which differs from the 44 dwarfs actually used in the original A19
analysis (Astra's dm_broad_dwarfs/results/dwarfs_benchmark.csv, built from McConnachie's January 2021
catalog). Ten of version 1's 27 dwarfs were in fact among the original 44. Version 2 corrects the
exclusion list and changes nothing else.

WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN
Before this file was written, the frozen rules had been scored on version 1's 27 dwarfs (17 of which are
outside the true original 44) and, in an earlier Local Volume Database rerun, on the dwarfs of Argon's list
that are outside the true original 44. The rules, coefficients, method and metric are unchanged from
version 1; only the exclusion list is corrected. This is therefore an out-of-sample test, and strictly
blind only for the 17 dwarfs scored under version 1.

CHANGE
Selection step 1 now reads: not one of the 44 dwarfs of the original analysis, listed by LVDB key in
original44_keys.txt (mapped from the system names in dwarfs_benchmark.csv). Steps 2-8, the frozen inputs,
the method, the primary and secondary outcomes, the bootstrap (20,000 resamples, seed 20261030) and the
sensitivities are exactly as in version 1. The 17-dwarf subset scored blind under version 1 is reported
separately.
