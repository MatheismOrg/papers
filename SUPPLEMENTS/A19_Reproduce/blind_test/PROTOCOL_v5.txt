A19 CLUSTER-SCALE TEST: ONE PUBLISHED HALO RELATION FROM DWARFS TO CLUSTERS
PROTOCOL VERSION 5
Written 31 October 2026, 01:10 US Pacific, by Claude (Opus 5.5), session SA73, turn 3, for Gary Abraham Bernstein.

PURPOSE
Section 5 of A19 shows that MOND's acceleration scale must grow about six times from disks to clusters.
The paper has not shown whether a halo set by starlight carries to cluster scales. The published Moster,
Naab and White (2013) relation, used unchanged for disks and dwarfs in Sections 3.2 to 3.5, maps a central
galaxy's stellar mass to its host halo's mass from about 10^10 to 10^15 suns. This test applies it, with
nothing fitted, to brightest cluster galaxies (BCGs) and their clusters.

WHAT WAS ALREADY SEEN
The cluster accelerations were scored in Section 5 against MOND's relation and freer formulas. Before this
file was written, the input tables were opened to check coverage (50 MaNGA BCGs with stellar mass, size,
redshift and one acceleration each; 20 CLASH clusters with BCG stellar mass, size, redshift and 84
accelerations), and Astra's archive was searched by name: it holds no prediction of these data from the
Moster relation or any halo model. No such prediction has been computed in this project.

DATA (copied to data/Clusters/, SHA-256 recorded in the results)
- MaNGA BCGs (Tian et al. 2024, A&A 684, A180, Table 1): manga_bcg_table1_extracted.txt for redshift,
  effective radius R_e, r_last/R_e and log10 stellar mass; manga_bcg_standardized.csv for the point at
  r = R_e (r_last/R_e) with g_bar, g_obs and their errors.
- CLASH (Tian et al. 2020, ApJ 896, 70; VizieR J/ApJ/896/70): clash_table1_idia.tsv for each BCG's
  redshift, R_e and stellar mass M* inside the BCG radius Rad (14.3 to 30.3 kpc); clash_standardized.csv for
  20 BCG-centred points and 64 points at 100, 200, 400 and 600 kpc.

MODELS (no fitting of any kind)
(H) Moster relation: M*(M_h, z) = 2 N M_h / [(M_h/M1)^(-beta) + (M_h/M1)^gamma], each parameter
    X(z) = X10 + X11 z/(1+z): log10 M1 = 11.590 + 1.195 z/(1+z), N = 0.0351 - 0.0247 z/(1+z),
    beta = 1.376 - 0.826 z/(1+z), gamma = 0.608 + 0.329 z/(1+z). Inverted for M_h at the BCG's stellar
    mass and redshift (search from 10^9 to 10^16.5 suns) and taken as M_200c, the larger of it and M*/f_b
    as A19 does. Halo: NFW truncated at r_200c(z); concentration of Dutton and Maccio (2014), NFW, Planck:
    log10 c = a + b log10(M_h h / 10^12), a = 0.520 + 0.385 exp(-0.617 z^1.21), b = -0.101 + 0.026 z;
    rho_crit(z) = 3 H(z)^2 / (8 pi G), H0 = 67.1 km/s/Mpc, Omega_m = 0.3175, Omega_Lambda = 0.6825.
    Dark mass (1 - f_b) M_h, f_b = 0.0490/0.3175. Prediction g = g_bar + g_NFW at each point's radius.
(R1) Radial acceleration relation with the disk a0 (1.0368e-10 m/s^2, frozen_disk_rules.json).
(R2) The same with the published a0 = 1.20e-10 m/s^2.
(S)  The stellar rule frozen on the disks (A, B, C), M_p = M*, R_e from the tables, with A19's z = 0 halo:
     a control that A19 Section 3.7 does not expect to carry above the Milky Way's mass.

SCORES
Residual = log10 g_obs - log10 g_pred. Each host weighs equally: a CLASH cluster's points share one
weight, and each MaNGA BCG has one. Score = root-mean-square residual over the 70 hosts, the square root of
the mean over hosts of each host's mean squared residual.
Primary: the score of (H) against (R1). Success: (H) lower, with the 95 percent interval of the score
difference from 5,000 resamples of hosts (seed 20261031) below zero.
Secondary: the same against (R2) and (S); the score, mean residual and median absolute fractional
acceleration error of every model in three groups (MaNGA BCGs; CLASH centres; CLASH at 100 to 600 kpc);
each CLASH cluster's residuals; each host's halo mass and concentration under (H).

SENSITIVITIES (reported, not used for the verdict)
CLASH BCG stellar masses doubled (the published values lie inside 14 to 30 kpc and miss outer light);
MaNGA BCG stellar masses lowered by 0.3 dex (a smaller aperture); redshift evolution switched off (z = 0
parameters and rho_crit, as A19 uses for dwarfs).

REPORTING
All outcomes are reported whatever they show, in the paper and the supplement. No model, constant, data
selection or score changes after this file's SHA-256 is recorded.
