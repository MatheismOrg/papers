A19 protocol 9: post-run correction (session SA73, turn 13, 31 October 2026, US Pacific)

This note was written after the run. It changes no protocol text, no hashed prediction and no verdict.

WHAT WAS WRONG
In code/A19_KiDS_v9.py, compare(), the Monte Carlo for the early/late split drew both profiles with the mean
vector ordered [early, late], while B21's covariance files for the colour and Sersic samples are ordered
[late, early] (bin 1, then bin 2). Each profile was therefore drawn with the other's covariance block. An outside
review found it after the run. It touches only sigma_S; the central values S_data and S_model, the amplitudes and
every chi-square are computed without the draws.

FIX
The draws now use the mean vector [late, early]. The results file also gives the first-order (delta-method) sigma
and the number of draws left out because a draw's mean early/late ratio is not positive: Gaussian draws of a noisy
late-type profile can cross zero, so the log ratio of the draws has heavy tails.

EFFECT (inside 0.3 Mpc / all 15 bins; before -> after)
Colour: standard deviation of the draws (the protocol's sigma_S) 0.040 -> 0.070 / 0.028 -> 0.076;
        68 percent half-width 0.040 -> 0.057 / 0.028 -> 0.044; delta method 0.048 / 0.034;
        draws left out 17 / 64 of 20,000.
Sersic: 0.041 -> 0.048 / 0.029 -> 0.042; 68 percent half-width 0.040 -> 0.046 / 0.029 -> 0.034;
        delta method 0.041 / 0.029; draws left out 3 / 5.
S_data (0.206 and 0.162 inside 0.3 Mpc; 0.275 and 0.194 over all bins), S_model and the fractions are unchanged.
With T = max(2 sigma_S, 0.25 S_data), T stays below |S_model - S_data| for every sample and for each of the three
sigmas above (colour inside 0.3 Mpc: T = 0.140, 0.113 or 0.096 against 0.157), so all four verdicts are unchanged:
DOES NOT for colour and ACCOUNTS FOR PART for Sersic, inside 0.3 Mpc and over all bins.
Because of the heavy tails, the delta-method values (0.048 and 0.041 inside 0.3 Mpc) are the ones quoted.
