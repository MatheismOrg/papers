pace2022_table3_pericenters.csv: pericenters of 46 Milky Way dwarf galaxies from Table 3 of Pace, A. B., Erkal, D.,
& Li, T. S. (2022), Proper motions, orbits, and tidal influences of Milky Way dwarf spheroidal galaxies, ApJ 940, 136,
doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ac997b, arXiv:2205.05699. Columns: pericenter with the LMC included in the potential (median
and 68 percent errors, kpc), the same without the LMC (the table's "nL" columns), and the p_LMC column as printed.
The paper defines the pericenter as the first local minimum of Galactocentric distance in the backward
integration. Transcribed 31 October 2026 (SA73, turn 8) from the arXiv PDF; two separate extractions agree on
every LMC-included pericenter. The LVDB key column maps each name to the Local Volume Database (Pace 2025); "Pis II"
is Pisces II. Not used by any protocol; used by A19_Pericenter_Check.py (descriptive).
