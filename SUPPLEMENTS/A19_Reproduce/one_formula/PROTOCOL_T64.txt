gas-free systems. Three kinds of dark-matter particle also change the inside of a halo, each by a rule that depends on the
halo and not on the gas, and each with one number that must be the same in every halo. Does any of them, with its number
set on the training disks and carried unchanged to every population, do what the gas-gated core does?
