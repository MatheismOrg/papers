A19 protocol T40 (session SA73, overnight round 20, 7 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before any HeCS value is read
(only the two table headers, column formats and labels, and the paper's text have been seen).
Blind test of the frozen T37 formula (FROZEN_T37_candidate.json, 9d0bc8d4...) on the HeCS-omnibus clusters.

DATA: Sohn, Geller, Diaferio and Rines (2020, ApJ 891, 129), machine-readable Tables 2 (clusters: zspec, M200, e_M200, R200,
Lx, Mx) and 3 (BCGs: Mass, e_Mass, VDISP), from content.cld.iop.org (apjab6e6at2_mrt.txt, apjab6e6at3_mrt.txt), read in
the browser pane, reduced in the page to two CSV files by the published byte ranges, with the SHA-256 of each full table
and each CSV recorded, and checked byte for byte after transfer. M200 and R200 are caustic values within 200 times the
critical density at the cluster's redshift (Section 3.2); BCG stellar masses are Le Phare fits to SDSS cModel photometry
with a Chabrier IMF (Section 2.2). None of these clusters' masses entered any A19 fit.
SAMPLE: every cluster with a BCG stellar mass (Mass not -99) and M200 > 0, joined on ID.

PREDICTION (blind_hecs_T40.py; nothing fitted): M_halo of the frozen T37 formula (A, B, C = 11.7481, 0.8178, -0.7119,
log10 M_t = 11.1768) for the BCG: M* = 10^Mass, M_p = M* (gas-poor), z = the cluster's zspec, R_e from a least-squares
line of log R_e on log M* over the 50 A19 MaNGA BCGs (the rule term is a few percent of M_halo for BCGs), selection
S = [M200c > 1e14 suns]: "most HeCS-omnibus clusters have ... dynamical masses >~ 10^14 M_sun" (Sohn et al. 2020, Sec. 3.2).
Published values as given (no conversion of h). Compared with log10 M200 (caustic).
Also scored, for reference: the same formula without selection (S = 1); the frozen T26 formula (Moster relation inverted
at its mean); the paper's rule alone; and a constant at the sample's median observed log M200 (not a prediction).
Reported: N, mean, median and rms of d = log10(M_pred/M200), its correlation with log M*, the same in acceleration at
R200 (log10 of the predicted NFW mass inside the measured R200 over M200, Dutton and Maccio concentration), the rms
against the MCXC X-ray masses where given (predicted M500c against Mx), and all of it without the clusters that are
also in A19's CLASH sample (name match).

TEST: the frozen T37 formula passes the blind test if (a) |mean d| <= 0.10 dex, (b) rms d <= 0.30 dex, and (c) its rms
is lower than both the formula without selection and the frozen T26 formula. All numbers reported whatever the outcome.
Sensitivity (reported, not part of the test): the threshold at 10^13.8 and 10^14.2 suns, and h = 0.70 to 0.671 conversions
of M* (x 1.088) and M200 (x 1.043).
