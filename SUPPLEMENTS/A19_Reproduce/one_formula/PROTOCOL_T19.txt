A19 cross-scale protocol T19 (session SA73, turn 19, 6 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T15 to T18. Adaptive (eighth round on seen data); not blind.

GOAL: a better and more united formula. Both T17b's size switch and every alternative tested so far either fit the
massive galaxies or the massive disks, not both, unless they can tell disks from spheroids. Lensing measured that
difference: at fixed stellar mass, passive central galaxies have halos at least twice as massive as star-forming ones
(Mandelbaum et al. 2016, MNRAS, doi 10.1093/mnras/stw188, log M* 10.3 to 11.6). The cold-gas fraction is this data's
measure of that: disks have 1.6 to 90 percent gas; dwarfs, early types, MaNGA and CLASH central galaxies and early-type
lenses are gas-poor (M_p = M*). In the existing data this is close to a population label for the switch; it is a
physical quantity that new data can measure.

All-data checks behind this protocol (reported, not held out): the gas-and-mass switch gave MaNGA 0.184 (size switch
0.213), clusters 0.196 (0.199), lensing 67.7 (68.4), disks 0.1498 (0.1495). Adding contraction where baryons dominate
(Cautun et al. 2020, contraction branch only) with the paper's rule kept as fitted on disks gave MaNGA 0.173 at s = 0.17.
The feedback-cored profile of Di Cintio et al. (2014) failed badly (disks 0.18, dwarfs 0.81 or worse), so it is not run.

FORMULA (harness_T19.py)
M_ref = Mp/fb + 10^A (M*/1e10)^B (Re/3 kpc)^C + S x M_Moster(M*, z)
A, B, C frozen on each fold's training disks (NFW, no contraction, no switch), as T17b.
MOG:  S = (M*/Mp)^50 / (1 + (M_t/M*)^4); one coefficient log10 M_t in [10, 12.5].
MOGC: MOG + contraction: M_DM(<r) = (1 - fb) M_NFW(<r) max(F(eta), 1)^s, F = 0.45 + 0.38 (eta + 1.16)^0.53,
      eta = M_bar(<r)/(fb M_NFW(<r)); s in [0, 1.5]; dynamics M_bar from g_bar, lensing M_p as a point mass.
MOSC: T17b's size switch (R_t) + the same contraction.
Concentration Dutton and Maccio (2014) at each redshift; Moline factor for satellites; M_Moster as T17b.
