F5 CLUSTER GALAXIES. The MaNGA brightest cluster galaxies' residuals rise with half-light radius (correlation +0.55, slope
+0.39 dex per dex) and fall with baryonic acceleration (-0.59). Size errors alone give such a trend only if they are about
0.08 dex and the kinematic errors are far below those quoted (noise52_manga_size.py). Forecast: with independent, deeper
half-light radii and stellar masses for the same 50 galaxies (for example the NASA-Sloan Atlas or deeper imaging), and the
stellar accelerations rebuilt from them, the correlation of the residuals with radius falls below 0.3. AGAINST: it stays
above 0.45, in which case the formula gives the stars too much weight in dense central galaxies.
