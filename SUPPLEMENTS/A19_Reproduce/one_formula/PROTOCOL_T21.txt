A19 cross-scale protocol T21 (session SA73, overnight round 2, 6 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T15 to T20. Adaptive (tenth round on seen data); not blind.

WHY: the dwarf errors are dominated by a few systems. The lightest dwarfs (rule halo masses below about 10^8.5 Msun,
32 of 75) sit on average 0.09 dex above the model (all data), and the most underpredicted ones (Carina III, Willman 1,
Segue 1, Eridanus IV, Horologium I) all have rule halo masses of 10^7.3 to 10^7.9 Msun, below the scale where the first
galaxies could form: a virial temperature of 1e4 K corresponds to about 1e7 to 1e8 Msun at z of 20 to 10 (Johnson 2013,
Formation of the first galaxies, eq. 1). All-data checks, reported, not held out: a floor on the halo mass at 1e8 Msun
lowers the dwarf error from 0.368 to 0.364 (10^8.5: 0.364; 10^9: 0.394); nothing else changes.
Tidal truncation at the Jacobi radius at pericentre (pericentres from Pace, Erkal and Li 2022, Table 3, read through the
browser on Gary's Mac, SHA-256 of the rows 610844...) changes almost nothing (0.3681 to 0.3674): only Antlia II is
truncated inside its half-light radius; Crater II's Jacobi radius equals its half-light radius. Not run as a protocol.

FORMULA (harness_T21.py): M_ref -> (M_ref^8 + M_min^8)^(1/8) on top of T19's formulas.
