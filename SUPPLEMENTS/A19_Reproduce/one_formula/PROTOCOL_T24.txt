A19 cross-scale protocol T24 (session SA73, overnight round 4, 7 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T15 to T22, except the one change below. Adaptive (twelfth
round on seen data); not blind. This round tests the baryons, not the halo formula.

WHY: with T21's MOGF, the MaNGA central galaxies sit 0.135 dex above the model on average (all data); the offset is
most of their error (rms 0.173). Massive early-type galaxies have heavier stellar mass-to-light ratios than a Chabrier
or Kroupa IMF gives, rising with velocity dispersion: log alpha = (0.38 +/- 0.04) log10(sigma_e/200 km/s) + (-0.06 +/-
0.01), alpha relative to Salpeter, rms 0.12 dex (Posacki et al. 2015, MNRAS 446, 493; ATLAS3D and SLACS). The MaNGA
masses (Tian et al. 2024, constant mass-to-light, r band 3.1 on average) are taken to be on a Chabrier-like scale.
Converting with Madau and Dickinson (2014): Chabrier masses / 0.61 and Kroupa masses / 0.66 give Salpeter masses.
At the median dispersion (272 km/s) the factor is 1.6 (Chabrier) or 1.5 (Kroupa).
All-data checks, reported, not held out (MOGF refitted on all data, primary mask): MaNGA 0.173 to 0.112 (mean +0.135 to
+0.009) for Chabrier, 0.114 (mean +0.032) for Kroupa; ETG 0.2012 to 0.2016, CLASH 0.1953 to 0.1956, lensing 67.89 to
67.83, disks and dwarfs unchanged; the contraction strength s falls from 0.173 to 0.136. Not fitted: no coefficient is
added, the IMF relation and conversions are published values.

CHANGE (harness_T24.py): the 50 MaNGA points' g_bar times alpha_IMF = 10^(0.38 log10(sigma/200) - 0.06 + delta), sigma
= each BCG's <sigma_los> (Tian et al. 2024, Table 1) for sigma_e; delta = 0.215 (Chabrier) or 0.180 (Kroupa). Stellar
masses in the halo formula stay on the published scale (as for the disks the rule was set on). The formula is T21's
MOGF, unchanged.

Both masks. All reported. Run on Gary's Mac (4-core Linux workspace) with chunk_run.py, as T21.

PASS MARKS
The IMF account passes if the held-out MaNGA error falls below MOGF@0.1's (T21) by more than the 5 percent tolerance
and no other score is worse beyond tolerance. Paired host bootstrap for MaNGA on the primary mask. A pass supports the
IMF reading of the offset; it does not change the halo formula. Caveat: the held-out MaNGA systems keep their measured
dispersions, so this tests a published relation, not a prediction from starlight alone.
