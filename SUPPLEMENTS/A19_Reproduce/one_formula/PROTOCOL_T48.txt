A19 protocol T48 (session SA73, round 26, written and hashed 2026-11-09 20:33 PST, US Pacific). Hashed before any held-out run of this
model. Gary's request (9 Nov, 20:31): "see how u might solve dm fit better across scales. if u can, do it."

WHY: the formula's first term, M_p/f_b, gives every halo at least the cosmic share for the baryons seen. For disks M_p is
stars plus cold gas. For the CLASH clusters M_p has been the brightest galaxy's stars alone, although the same data give
the clusters' hot gas, most of their baryons. T45 leaves the clusters at 100 to 600 kpc 0.11 dex low on average.

ALL-DATA LOOK BEFORE THIS PROTOCOL (explore47_gas.py; not a test): with M_p = all baryons inside each cluster's outermost
measured radius, CLASH 100 to 600 kpc 0.1323 -> 0.1002 dex (mean offset +0.114 -> +0.068), centers 0.1055 -> 0.1020.
A constant ratio g_obs/g_bar (no formula) has 0.11 to 0.16 dex scatter at fixed radius, so the gain is not the gas alone.

MODEL MOGF0IESB (harness_T48.py): T45's MOGF0IESM in every respect, except that for each CLASH cluster
M_p = max(M_star, g_bar r^2 / G at the cluster's outermost benchmark radius), the stars and hot gas inside that radius
(Tian et al. 2020 g_bar). Nothing fitted; no other population changes (MaNGA centrals have no measured hot gas).
Coefficients fitted to the benchmark: 3 (A, B, C on the training disks), as in T45.

RUN: chunk_split.py, the same 60 partitions, main and stricter masks, config MOGF0IESB@0.1. Compare with T45 held out.

PASS (preset): on both masks every population within its tolerance of T45 (disks 1 percent, dwarfs 2, early types, MaNGA,
CLASH centers 5, lensing 5), and CLASH 100 to 600 kpc lower than T45 with a host-bootstrap 95 percent interval of the
difference below zero. If it passes, T48 replaces T45 as the final formula; otherwise T45 stays and T48 is reported as tried.

CAVEATS FIXED IN ADVANCE: retrospective (found on these data); M_p depends on the aperture of the data (400 to 600 kpc);
the HeCS blind test cannot test it (no gas); the sum M_p/f_b + M_E is the formula's own form, not a derivation.
