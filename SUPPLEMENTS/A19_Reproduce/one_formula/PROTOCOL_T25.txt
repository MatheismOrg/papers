WHY: T24 passed on the primary mask (held out: MaNGA 0.1726 to 0.1113 with the Chabrier factor, nothing worse beyond
tolerance). Once the MaNGA baryons carry the IMF factor, the contraction term of T19 buys almost nothing. All-data checks,
reported, not held out (primary mask, refitted): MOGF + IMF (5 coefficients): disks 0.1496, dwarfs 0.3639, ETG 0.2016,
MaNGA 0.1122, CLASH 0.1956, lensing 67.83; MOGF0 + IMF (no contraction, 4 coefficients): 0.1498, 0.3639, 0.2026,
0.1141, 0.1961, 67.69. Without the IMF factor, dropping contraction costs MaNGA 0.173 to 0.184. So T19's contraction
mostly absorbed the MaNGA IMF offset. A formula with one coefficient fewer that matches is preferred.

FORMULA (harness_T25.py): T21's MOGF0: M_ref = Mp/fb + 10^A (M*/1e10)^B (Re/3)^C + q M_Moster(M*, z),

satellite factor, no contraction. MaNGA g_bar x alpha_IMF as T24 (Chabrier, delta = 0.215).
