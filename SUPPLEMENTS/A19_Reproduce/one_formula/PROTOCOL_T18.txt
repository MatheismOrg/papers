WHY: under T17b's best formula (MOSS) the big central galaxies are underpredicted on average (MaNGA host-mean
residual +0.18 dex at 0.6 to 0.9 Re; early-type inner points +0.13). All-data checks, reported, not held out:
Blumenthal contraction removes the bias (MaNGA 0.213 to 0.110) but ruins disks (0.150 to 0.167 even refitted);
the simulation-calibrated contraction of Cautun et al. (2020, eq. 11) at full strength improves MaNGA (0.141),
early types (0.187), clusters (0.181) and dwarfs (0.362) but costs disks 4 percent (0.155); at strength 0.5 disks
are unchanged (0.1496) and all four others still improve. Contraction raised lensing chi-square at fixed coefficients
(68.4 to 74.9 at strength 0.5).

FORMULA: T17b's MOSS (paper's rule + Moster mass for galaxies larger than R_t, Dutton-Maccio c at each redshift,
Moline factor for satellites), with the dark matter contracted:
  M_DM(<r) = (1 - fb) M_NFW(<r) F(eta)^s,  F(eta) = 0.45 + 0.38 (eta + 1.16)^0.53,  eta = M_bar(<r) / (fb M_NFW(<r)),
M_NFW the uncontracted total NFW mass profile (dark-matter-only), fb = 0.049/0.3175 (Cautun et al. 2020, Auriga, APOSTLE
and EAGLE, haloes near 1e12 Msun; applied here at all masses, an extrapolation).
Dynamics: M_bar(<r) = g_bar r^2 / G at the data radius (disks: stars and cold gas; CLASH outer points include hot gas).
Lensing: the lens's M_p as a point mass (all bins lie beyond 4 lens effective radii); projection by Gauss-Legendre
quadrature in r = R cosh t, checked against Wright and Brainerd at s = 0 (1e-12) and brute force at s = 1 (1e-5).
The paper's rule A, B, C is refitted on each fold's training disks WITH contraction and without the switch;
then R_t is fitted as in T17b (w = 0.1).
