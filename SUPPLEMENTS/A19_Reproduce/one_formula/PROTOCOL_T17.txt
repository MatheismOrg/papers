A19 cross-scale protocol T17 (session SA73, turn 17, 6 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Builds on T16's best formula, SUBH (paper's rule frozen on training disks + high-mass branch + Dutton-Maccio
concentration at each halo's redshift + Moline subhalo concentration). Same data, partitions, objective, scores,
tolerances. Adaptive (fifth round on seen data); not blind.

TWO PHYSICAL ADDITIONS
1. Published redshift evolution of the high-mass branch, no new coefficient: the branch is multiplied by
   M_Moster(M*, z) / M_Moster(M*, 0), the halo-mass ratio at fixed stellar mass from Moster, Naab and White (2013)
   with its published evolution (A19_Clusters_v5.moster_halo, evolve=True). Zero at z = 0. Replaces T16's free
   (1+z)^D2, which ran into its bound.
2. Gas-rich galaxies in less concentrated halos, one coefficient: log10 c gains f x (Mp - M*)/Mp, the cold-gas
   fraction. Gas-poor systems (dwarfs, early types, cluster centrals) are unaffected. Late-forming halos are less
   concentrated, and gas-rich galaxies formed late. f in [-1, 1].
