A19 cross-scale protocol T27 (session SA73, overnight round 7, 7 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T26, except that MaNGA and CLASH are left out of every
training set. Adaptive (fifteenth round on seen data); not blind across rounds, but out-of-population within the round.

WHY: in T19 to T26 the switch threshold M_t was fitted with MaNGA and CLASH in the training sets (held-out hosts only
were scored). A stronger test: set M_t without any brightest cluster galaxy or cluster, from disks, dwarfs, the 16
early types and the KiDS lenses alone, then predict every MaNGA and CLASH system. All-data check, reported, not held
out (primary mask): without MaNGA and CLASH, log10 M_t = 11.211 (11.187 with them); MaNGA 0.1146 (0.1141 when trained
on), CLASH 0.1970 (0.1962), early types 0.1853, lensing 67.66, disks 0.1498, dwarfs 0.3639.

RUNNER (chunk_run_lopo.py): chunk_run.py with one change, MaNGA and CLASH deleted from each training set; the model is
registered under the alias MOGF0IEX so results do not mix with trained runs. The disk rule A, B, C is fitted on each
fold's training disks as before. Scores for MaNGA and CLASH use the same 60 partitions and host pooling as T26, so every
MaNGA and CLASH score is an out-of-population prediction.
CONFIGURATION
MOGF0IEX@0.1  through harness_T26.py (T26's formula and baryons; 4 coefficients, M_t fitted without MaNGA and CLASH)
Both masks. Run on Gary's Mac with chunk_run_lopo.py, or in the cloud with the same files.

PASS MARKS
The big-galaxy branch counts as predicted if the out-of-population MaNGA, CLASH outer and CLASH center errors are each
within the 5 percent tolerance of MOGF0IE@0.1 (T26, trained on them), on both masks. Other scores are reported.
