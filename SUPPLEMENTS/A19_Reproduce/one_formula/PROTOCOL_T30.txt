A19 protocol T30 (session SA73, overnight round 10, 7 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before any prediction.
Out-of-sample test of the frozen T26 formula (FROZEN_T26_candidate.json, SHA-256 eaecf32e...) against the galaxy-galaxy
lensing halo masses of SDSS central galaxies (Mandelbaum et al. 2016, MNRAS 457, 3200; data/Lensing/
mandelbaum2016_central_halos.csv of the A19 package, as in the paper's eighth protocol). No overnight fit used these data;
they motivated the gas-poor switch qualitatively (passive centrals sit in heavier halos) but set no number.

PREDICTIONS (diag_mandelbaum.py). For each red and blue bin: stellar mass = the VAGC effective mass (the paper's
canonical choice), redshift = the bin's effective lens redshift. Blue (star-forming) centrals carry cold gas from the
disks' HI-mass scaling and the disks' size scaling, as in the paper's eighth protocol. Red (passive) centrals have no
cold gas, so the switch's gas factor is 1, and half the disks' size at the same stellar mass, as in the paper's eighth
protocol; the disks' size is reported as a sensitivity case. Halo: the frozen formula's M_halo with A, B, C = 11.7481,
0.8178, -0.7119 and log10 M_t = 11.1868, NFW with the Dutton and Maccio (2014) concentration at the bin's redshift,
converted from 200 times critical to 200 times mean density in h^-1 suns (h = 0.673) with colossus, as the paper does.
Mandelbaum's values are lensing-weighted mean halo masses, which exceed typical masses by roughly 0.05 to 0.1 dex; the
comparison leaves this uncorrected, as the paper does.

TESTS (all reported)
R: red centrals, all seven bins (10^10.28 to 10^11.68 suns): each within 0.2 dex or twice its error.
B: blue centrals below 10^11.4 suns (the paper's criterion A): each within 0.2 dex or twice its error.
S: the red-to-blue halo-mass split at 10^10.7 to 10^11.2 suns, predicted against measured.
For comparison only: the paper's rule (no switch) and the Moster relation alone, from the paper's eighth-protocol code.
