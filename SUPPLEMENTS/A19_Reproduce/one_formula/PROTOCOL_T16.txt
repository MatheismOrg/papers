A19 cross-scale protocol T16 (session SA73, turn 16, 6 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Builds on T15c's best formula, KNEESFZ: the paper's rule frozen on training disks, plus the high-mass branch
10^A2 (M*/1e11)^B2 (Re/10 kpc)^C2, with the Dutton and Maccio (2014) concentration at each halo's redshift.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T15. Adaptive (fourth round on seen data); not blind.

TWO PHYSICAL ADDITIONS, EACH PUBLISHED OR STANDARD
1. Subhalo concentration (no new coefficient): a satellite inside its host's r200 has its concentration multiplied
   by 1 - 0.54 log10(D/r200,host) (Moline et al. 2017), exactly as in protocol 4's exploratory variant E1
   (A19_New_Dwarfs_v3.predict: Milky Way 1.0e12, M31 1.5e12 suns). Only dwarf satellites are affected.
2. Redshift evolution of the high-mass branch (one coefficient): the branch is multiplied by (1+z)^D2, as abundance
   matching finds central galaxies of given stellar mass in heavier halos at higher redshift. D2 in [-3, 3].
