FORECAST_T60 (SA73 T60). Written 15:09 PST, 10 Nov 2026, after FORECAST_T59 was run and before anything below was computed.

Where things stand. FORECAST_T59 as worded failed: with the benchmark's cut on velocity errors (10 percent) only three of
the eight outside dwarf irregulars keep any point, and on those T57's error is 3.6 percent higher than T48's (paired
range -0.020 to +0.033, not significant). After the fact, with the cut loosened so that all eight enter (86 points), T57
is lower in seven of eight, by 13 percent (range -0.085 to -0.021). That variant was chosen after the worded result and
is a lead, not a test. Two tests of it follow, fixed here.

Part A, run now: the SPARC points that the benchmark never scored. The benchmark keeps points with velocity error at most
10 percent of the velocity. The points it drops (error above 10 percent, positive velocity, positive baryonic V^2), in all
175 SPARC galaxies, were not used to fit anything, the two numbers of T57 included; but their galaxies were, so Part A is
not an outside test, only a test on unused points. T48 and T57 frozen (A, B, C = 11.7481, 0.8178, -0.7119; t = 0.361,
s = 0.146), SPARC's own inputs, host-balanced rms of log10(g_obs / g_pred) over the galaxies with at least one such
point. Forecast A: T57's error is lower than T48's, and the 95 percent range of the paired bootstrap difference over
galaxies (10,000 draws) excludes zero. A higher error counts against the term; a lower error with a range that includes
zero leaves it open. Reported alongside, without a forecast: the same for the galaxies in the benchmark and for those
excluded from it separately, for galaxies with g L above the median of the nonzero values, and for MOND's relation and
standard halos.

Part B, a seventh forecast for data not in hand (F7). On at least ten gas-rich dwarf irregular or low-surface-brightness
disk galaxies that are in neither SPARC nor Iorio et al. (2017), with resolved HI rotation curves corrected for asymmetric
drift, 3.6 micron luminosities and sizes, all points kept whatever their velocity error, and inputs built as in
FORECAST_T59 (stellar mass 0.5 L[3.6], gas 1.33 HI, M_p their sum): T57's host-balanced error is lower than T48's and the
95 percent range of the paired bootstrap difference over galaxies excludes zero. If T57's error is higher, the term is
wrong; if it is lower without excluding zero, it stays open.

The formula of the paper stays T48 whatever the outcome of either part.
