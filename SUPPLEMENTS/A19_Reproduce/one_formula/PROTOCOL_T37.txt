A19 protocol T37 (session SA73, overnight round 17, 7 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before any run.
Does the expected halo given a galaxy's starlight and its sample's selection work as the formula's high-mass branch,
held out, with the threshold refitted?

WHY: T31 to T34 (all data, frozen coefficients, nothing fitted) found that one relation, the Moster et al. (2013)
relation inverted with its scatter and the halo mass function, restricted to each sample's published halo selection,
fits the SDSS red centrals of Mandelbaum et al. (2016) far better than the mean inversion, fits CLASH better (0.144 and
0.107 dex at 100 to 600 kpc and at the centers, against 0.207 and 0.155 for the frozen formula) and keeps MaNGA within
tolerance (0.120 against 0.114). Those were diagnostics at the frozen threshold M_t, and the CLASH and MaNGA ones
averaged the prediction over the halo posterior. T37 builds the relation into the formula as one expected halo per
galaxy (the posterior mean mass, as T31), refits M_t, and scores it held out.

MODEL MOGF0IES (harness_T37.py; 4 coefficients, as T26)
  M_ref = M_p/f_b + 10^A (M*/1e10)^B (R_e/3 kpc)^C + q M_E(M*, z | S),   q = (M*/M_p)^50 / [1 + (M_t/M*)^4],
  M_halo = (M_ref^8 + (1e8 suns)^8)^(1/8); NFW, Dutton and Maccio (2014) concentration, Moline subhalo factor for
  satellite dwarfs, no contraction; the IMF factors on the baryons of the MaNGA BCGs and the 16 early types as T26.
  M_E is the posterior mean of M200c: the sum over a grid in log10 M200c from 9.0 to 16.5 (step 0.005) of M w(M),
  divided by the sum of w(M), with w(M) = dn/dlnM (Tinker et al. 2008, colossus, the A19 Planck cosmology as T32)
  x exp(-0.5 [(log10 M* - log10 M*_Moster(M, z)) / sigma]^2) x S(M), sigma = sqrt(0.15^2 + 0.10^2) dex (as T31 to T34).
  S(M) = 1 if M200c > 5e14 suns, else 0, for the 20 CLASH clusters (Postman et al. 2012, as T32); S(M) = 1 if
  M200c > 1e14 suns, else 0, for the 50 MaNGA BCGs (the MaNGA BCG program's aim, as T33); S = 1 for every other sample
  (disks, dwarfs, early types and KiDS lenses, chosen by their light or not by their halos).
  Below M* = 10^9.5 suns, M_E is the mean inversion used by T26 (the switch is below 1e-6 there).
  Unchanged from T26: A, B, C fitted on each fold's training disks without the switch; log10 M_t fitted jointly on all
  training systems at population weight 0.1, bounds 10 to 12.5.

RUNS
1. All-data check (describe24.py, primary mask) for T26 (MOGF0IE) and T37 (MOGF0IES); both reported.
2. Held out: 60 partitions (3 disk-fold assignments x 5 folds x 4 KiDS lensing groups) on the primary and the stricter
   mask (chunk_split.py; same fold maps, fits and losses as T26). Run whatever step 1 shows.

TEST (held out, against T26's frozen formula MOGF0IE on the same 60 partitions)
T37 passes if, on both masks, every population's score is within its tolerance of T26's (disks 1 percent, dwarfs
2 percent, all others 5 percent, the lensing chi-square 5 percent), and the CLASH errors at 100 to 600 kpc and at the
centers are each lower than T26's by more than 5 percent with paired host-bootstrap 95 percent intervals of the change
below zero. If it passes, the formula's high-mass branch becomes T37's (no coefficient added). If any population is
worse beyond its tolerance, it fails; the scores are reported either way, with every configuration run.
