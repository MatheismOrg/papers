FORECAST_T59 (SA73 T59). Written 15:07 PST, 10 Nov 2026, before any outside galaxy is scored.

Question. Protocol T57 added one term to the final formula (T48): for light-selected systems, with g = M_gas / M_p and
L = -log10 min(Sigma_* / Sigma_p, 1), the halo mass is multiplied by 10^(t g L) and its concentration by 10^(-s g L). Held
out on the benchmark it lowered the disk error by 1.3 percent and missed its preset mark; on the 17 SPARC galaxies excluded
from the benchmark (FORECAST_T58) it lowered the error by 2.3 percent (one-sided p 0.08). This file tests the term, with
t = 0.361 and s = 0.146 frozen from the 149 benchmark disks, on dwarf irregulars that are not in SPARC.

Data. Iorio et al. (2017, MNRAS 466, 4159) publish asymmetric-drift-corrected circular velocities and intrinsic HI surface
densities for 17 LITTLE THINGS galaxies (finalrot.zip, sha256 d4c456c6...). Seven are in SPARC (DDO 50 = UGC04305, DDO 87 =
UGC05918, DDO 126 = UGC07559, DDO 154, DDO 168, NGC 2366, WLM = UGCA444) and serve only to check the inputs. Of the other
ten, DDO 47 and NGC 1569 have no 3.6 micron flux in the LVL survey (Dale et al. 2009, Table 2). The test sample is the
remaining eight: CVnIdwA, DDO 52, DDO 53, DDO 101, DDO 133, DDO 210, DDO 216 (first of its two tables), UGC 8508, as many
as keep at least one point after the cuts. Clean subsample: the four not flagged by Read et al. (2017, MNRAS 467, 2019,
Table 2) for uncertain inclination, disequilibrium or distance: CVnIdwA, DDO 52, DDO 210, UGC 8508.

Inputs, on the scale of SPARC (model59.py). Distances and inclinations of Iorio et al. Stellar mass 0.5 L[3.6], with L[3.6]
from the LVL flux (zero point 280.9 Jy, solar magnitude 3.24), divided by KL = 1.172. Half-light radius 1.678 R_*, with R_*
the exponential scale length of Read et al. (2017), divided by KR = 1.424. KL and KR are the medians, over the seven shared
galaxies, of these quantities over SPARC's own (validate59.py, res59_validate.json: input ratios only, no formula; the
ratios scatter from 0.5 to 5 in luminosity and 0.7 to 3.1 in radius, the largest for WLM and NGC 2366). Gas: razor-thin
disk from the HI surface density times 1.33, flat inside the first point, linear between points, exponential beyond the last
point with the slope of the outer half of the profile; M_gas = 1.33 times the HI mass of that model (median 0.97 of SPARC's
on the shared galaxies, not rescaled). Stars: razor-thin exponential disk. M_p = M_* + M_gas.

Cuts, as in the benchmark. Inclination at least 30 degrees; points with velocity error at most 10 percent of the velocity,
positive velocity and positive baryonic V^2.

Formulas, all frozen. T48: A, B, C = 11.7481, 0.8178, -0.7119. T57: the same with t = 0.361, s = 0.146. Score: host-balanced
rms of log10(g_obs / g_pred). Script test59.py.

Forecast. (1) On the test sample T57's error is lower than T48's. (2) On the clean subsample T57's error is lower than T48's.
A higher error on the test sample counts against the term.
Combined mark. With the 17 galaxies of FORECAST_T58 added (their result is already known, so this mark is not blind to
them), the term counts as supported outside the benchmark only if the 95 percent range of the paired bootstrap difference
over galaxies (10,000 draws) excludes zero. Otherwise it stays a lead that is not secure.

Reported without a forecast: the same with KL = KR = 1; with the gas profile cut half a step beyond the last point; with
the rotation velocities before the asymmetric-drift correction; MOND's relation (a0 = 1.2e-10 m/s^2) and standard
abundance-matched halos on the same galaxies, as in Table 8; mean offsets and within-galaxy scatter; the seven shared
galaxies scored with these inputs and with SPARC's own.

The formula of the paper stays T48 whatever the outcome; T57 missed its preset mark and is not adopted.
