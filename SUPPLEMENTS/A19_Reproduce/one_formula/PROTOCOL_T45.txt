A19 protocol T45 (session SA73, round 25, written and hashed 2026-11-07 10:39 PST, US Pacific). Hashed before any held-out run of
this model. Gary's request: keep improving the one formula ("continue", 10:15 PST).

WHY: since T43, samples chosen by their halos (CLASH, MaNGA BCGs) take the expected halo whatever M_t, so the gate
threshold M_t, the one coefficient beyond the disk rule, is set only by the 16 early types and the KiDS stacks, and
weakly (T44 held out: log10 M_t from 10.89 to the 12.5 bound, 1 and 10 bound hits on the two masks). The gate stands
for the measured halo excess of passive central galaxies (Mandelbaum et al. 2016). Setting M_t on those published
central halo masses instead of the benchmark makes every benchmark population other than the disks a prediction with
nothing fitted to it.

ALL-DATA LOOK BEFORE THIS PROTOCOL (explore45.py, res45_explore.json; not a test): calibrated on the seven red bins
with the all-disk A, B, C, log10 M_t = 11.339 (Delta chi2 = 1 range 11.32 to 11.35; chi2 61.2 for 7 bins, so the formula
does not follow the red-central relation closely). At that M_t the all-data scores equal T44's within 0.1 percent except
lensing (63.19 against 61.83 on the main mask, 38.14 against 39.69 on the stricter mask). A mixture form of the halo,
q M_E + (1 - q)(M_p/f_b + rule), was looked at too (M_t 11.267 from Mandelbaum; CLASH outer 0.1337 against 0.1323,
nothing better), so it is not taken forward.

MODEL MOGF0IESM (harness_T45.py): T44's MOGF0IESR in every respect (halo formula, M_E with each sample's selection,
q = 1 for halo-selected samples, IMF factor, floor, Moline factor, KiDS all-halo ratio), except that log10 M_t is not
fitted to the benchmark. In each partition, after A, B, C are fitted on the training disks (as in every round),
log10 M_t is the value minimizing chi2 = sum over the seven red-central bins of Mandelbaum et al. (2016, MNRAS 457,
3200; package file data/Lensing/mandelbaum2016_central_halos.csv) of ((pred - obs)/err)^2, err = err_plus if
pred > obs else err_minus, where pred = log10 M200m [h^-1 M_sun] of the formula's halo for a red central (M_p = M*,
R_e = 0.5 x the SPARC size relation, z = z_eff, no selection), converted from M200c with the Dutton and Maccio
concentration by A19_Lensing_v8.to_m200m, exactly as in the T38 check; grid 10.0 to 12.5 in steps of 0.01, refined
to 0.001 about the minimum. A minimum on a grid edge counts as a bound hit. No benchmark system enters M_t.
Coefficients: 3 fitted to the benchmark (A, B, C on the training disks); M_t set on external lensing data.

RUN: chunk_split.py, the same 60 partitions, main and stricter masks, config MOGF0IESM@0.1 (the weight is unused).
Compare with T44 held out (res44_MOGF0IESR_primary.json, res44_MOGF0IESR_strict.json).

PASS (preset): on both masks, every population's held-out score within its tolerance of T44 (disks 1 percent,
dwarfs 2, early types, MaNGA, CLASH centers and CLASH 100 to 600 kpc 5, lensing chi2 5). If it passes, T45 replaces
T44 as the final formula and Section 5.4's first limit is rewritten; if any population fails, T44 stays and T45 is
reported as tried.

REPORT: every score on both masks; log10 M_t median and range over partitions; bound hits; host bootstrap of the
T45 - T44 differences; the red-central chi2 and rms at the all-disk M_t. Secondary, not pass criteria: the KiDS
early/late split (S_model) and the Milky Way's switch q.
