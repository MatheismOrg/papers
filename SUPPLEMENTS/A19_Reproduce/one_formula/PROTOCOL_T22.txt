A19 cross-scale protocol T22 (session SA73, overnight round 3, 6 November 2026, US Pacific). Hashed before running.
Same data, partitions, objective, scores and tolerances as T15 to T21. Adaptive (eleventh round on seen data); not blind.

WHY: every halo should hold its cosmic share of baryons. The galaxy's stars and cold gas (Mp) fall short of fb M_ref;
the rest is ejected or sits in a hot halo. The Milky Way's hot halo has density ~ r^-1.5 (beta = 0.50 +/- 0.03) and holds
at most about half of its missing baryons within 250 kpc (Miller and Bregman 2015, ApJ 800, 14). For central galaxies of
groups (MaNGA), the group's hot gas is missing from their g_bar too; for CLASH, g_bar already includes the intracluster gas.
All-data check, reported, not held out: half the missing baryons in an r^-1.5 hot halo (CLASH excluded) improves MaNGA
0.173 to 0.160 and lensing 67.9 to 66.8, disks 0.1496 to 0.1499, dwarfs 0.3681 to 0.3686; all of them: MaNGA 0.149,
lensing 68.0, disks 0.1505. Projection checked against brute force to 1e-14.

FORMULA (harness_T22.py): T19's MOGC plus g_hot = G M_hot(<r)/r^2, M_hot(<r) = f (fb M_ref - Mp) min(r/r200, 1)^1.5,
no hot term at CLASH points; lensing ESD of the same truncated r^-1.5 sphere added to the dark matter.
