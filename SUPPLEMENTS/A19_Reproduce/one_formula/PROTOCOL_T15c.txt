A19 cross-scale protocol T15c: second and last adaptive follow-up (session SA73, turn 15, 6 November 2026).
Written after T15 and T15b and hashed before running. Same data, partitions, objective, scores and pass marks.

WHY
All T15 and T15b formulas take the halo concentration from the z = 0 relation of Dutton and Maccio (2014),
c = 10^0.905 (M h/1e12)^-0.101, even for the KiDS lenses (z = 0.25) and the CLASH clusters (z = 0.19 to 0.89).
The published relation lowers concentration with redshift. Here every halo uses it at its own redshift:
log10 c = 0.520 + 0.385 exp(-0.617 z^1.21) + (-0.101 + 0.026 z) log10(M h/1e12). It is identical at z = 0, so
disks, dwarfs and early types are unchanged at fixed coefficients, and it adds no coefficient.

CONFIGURATIONS (suffix Z = redshift-dependent concentration)
ORIGZ@disk; KNEESFZ@0.1 and @1 (paper's rule frozen on training disks + high-mass branch); KNEESZ@0.1;
KNEESEZ@0.1. Both masks. Everything reported. Adaptive choices on reused data; this is the last round this turn.
