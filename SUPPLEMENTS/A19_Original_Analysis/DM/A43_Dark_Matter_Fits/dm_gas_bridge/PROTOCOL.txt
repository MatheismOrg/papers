A43 uniform-gas coupling bridge stress test, 29 October 2026.
Frozen before fitting this stage; all targets were examined in earlier A43 work.
This is an exploratory compatibility test, not independent discovery.

Question: can the large gas-associated source coefficient inferred in clusters
also describe neutral-gas-rich disk regions with the same constants?
The cluster prescription is existing prior art, Famaey,Pizzuti,Saltas2025 Eq17:
rho_extra=eta*rho_hot*exp(-r/Rc), a0=1.2e-10 m/s2.
The proposed test extension to disks uses a constant gas-density multiplier,
giving exactly g_src=g_stars+(1+eta)*g_gas for the supplied gas-force geometry.
It is the leading uniform-weight limit, NOT the full finite-Rc convolution for
a flattened disk and NOT what the published hot-gas-only proposal asserts.
An evaluation radius <=30kpc alone cannot bound its convolution error; sources
outside that radius also contribute. No unsupported finite-cutoff error bound
or exclusion of the published model will be claimed.

Data: tmp/dm_halo_benchmark/matched_components.csv supplies the unchanged2700
SPARC measurements and independent gas/stellar force contributions. Primary
predictor-selected subset: positive gas inward force, gas contribution >=0.5
of the total baryonic inward force, and physical radius<=30kpc. This selects
402rows in61galaxies. No observed velocity/target is used for this additional
selection. Raw original SPARC quality selection remains inherited.
Use all86 frozen cluster points in23hosts from dm_cluster_repair, with their
unchanged numerical quadrature of the hot-gas profile. No new astronomical data.
The joint sample has488rows in84systems. Missing hot gas elsewhere is not set
to zero; this test explicitly compares measured cold and hot gas components.

The calculation has a single source coefficient and scale: g_src=g_bar+eta*K.
K is measured g_gas for the constant-weight disk approximation; for spherical
clusters K=G/r^2 integral_0^r exp(-s/Rc)dM_hot(s). Different source geometry
requires different numerical kernels, not different fitted eta values.
Use simple MOND nu(y)=.5+sqrt(.25+1/y) and exponential RAR as two prespecified
interpolation choices, each with fixed a0. No interpolation-shape search.

Frozen comparators: eta=0; illustrative literature eta=10,Rc=400kpc;
CLASH-trained eta,Rc from previous exact simple/exp source-exclusion fit for
X-COP. The frozen coefficients were informed by CLASH, so scores on CLASH are
descriptive; their disk predictions are parameter transfer, not modelselection
independent discovery. All fitted comparators exclude testtargets.

Fits: disk-only common eta in [0,1000], deterministic starts0,1,10. Rc does not
enter disk approximation and is not fitted there. Cluster-only and joint fits
common [eta,log10(Rc/kpc)], bounds [0,1] to[1000,4], starts
[.1,2],[1,2.6],[10,2.6],[100,1.5]. Select lowest cost converged start, retain all
attempts/bounds/coefficients/failures. scipy least_squares max_nfev2000,
ftol=xtol=gtol=1e-11. No additional family or bound changes after scores.

Weights: joint disk and cluster domains receive half totalweight each. Within
clusters give CLASH and X-COP equal totals, then equal hosts and equal points
within host. Within disks give equal galaxies and equal points within galaxy.
If fitting/evaluating one domain or source alone, normalize only represented
domains and sources. Loss is weighted squared log10 acceleration residual.
Report overall RMSdex, domain/source/galaxy metrics and median fractional
acceleration/speed errors (speed only physically interpreted for disk rows).

Validation: all-data fits; five folds holding out entire hosts/galaxies,
stratified by source, seeds20261027,7331,48105. Each model separately trains on
disk-only, cluster-only, and joint inputs. Also joint source exclusions for
CLASH and X-COP (all matching hosts excluded); and entire disk-domain exclusion
(fit clusters only, predict all selected disks). Cold-to-cluster transfer uses
disk-trained eta and the externally fixed published Rc=400kpc, explicitly not
a disk-estimated length. No unseen-cluster prediction is claimed without that
additional declared length assumption. Retain all variants/splits.

Full2700-row diagnostics apply frozen and all-data coefficients, preserve
nonpositive g_src as invalid rows, and never clip them or silently drop them
from a claimed whole-sample score. Report valid-row conditional metrics alongside
invalid counts and names. Primary subset has positive K and g_bar so all fitted
nonnegative eta values produce positive source forces.

Descriptive uncertainty: paired5000 independent galaxy/host resamplings of frozen
prediction errors, preserving domain/source weights, seed20261029; no refit or
selection/measurement-systematic uncertainty. Comparison of joint and separately
calibrated CV measures an empirical tension, not a theorem about all gas physics.
Cold/hot differing preferred coefficients would not derive temperature dependence
or gravitational memory. Positive extra source mass has no asserted microscopic
identity. No Solar external-field, merger, cosmological, or full disk-field fit.
