A43 physical-source bridge: positive convolution kernels, 29 October 2026
Written before this run. Exploratory reuse of previously inspected data.

Start from published nonlocal gravity (NLG) and constant-parameter weak-field
MOG/STVG, not a new curve family. In the spherical isolated Newtonian regime:
 Laplacian Phi=4 pi G(rho_b+q*rho_b).
 NLG q(d)=alpha mu(1+mu d) exp(-mu d)/(8 pi d^2).
 MOG q(d)=alpha mu^2 exp(-mu d)/(4 pi d).
Both kernels are nonnegative and integrate to alpha. Frozen galaxy/cluster
parameters are NLG alpha=10.94,mu=.059/kpc (Rahvar & Mashhoon2014), and
MOG alpha=8.89,mu=.042/kpc (Moffat & Rahvar2014). These are not new measurements
or every variant of either theory. The NLG kernel here is its zero-small-length
astronomical limit; Solar work includes an additional short-distance parameter.

Rigorous diagnostic independent of the radial shape: for an isolated finite
positive baryon source, total effective extra mass is alpha M_b. In spherical
symmetry, at a baryonic half-mass radius, g/g_b <=1+2alpha. This is a necessary
condition, not a sufficient successful fit. The data use nominal stellar mass
M/L_V=2 and Wolf half-light acceleration estimates; inspect M/L=1 and3 as the
same predeclared sensitivity. The observed dispersions, equilibrium, stellar
population masses and baryon-only mass accounting remain assumptions. No
confidence-level exclusion of a complete theory is claimed.

Conditional source model: spherical Plummer baryons, total M_star from the same
input and a=r_half/sqrt(1/(2^(2/3)-1)), hence M(<r_half)=M_star/2 exactly. Full
source convolution includes baryons outside r_half; never substitute an enclosed
point mass in the modified-force law. Integrate the normalized kernel's radial
probability density p(t) (t=mu d): .5(1+t)exp(-t) for NLG and t exp(-t) for MOG,
against the Plummer mass inside a sphere of radius r_half displaced by d. At
r_half, g/g_b=1+2alpha H(mu a), where H is that integral. Tabulate H on401log
spaced z values1e-8..1e8, PCHIP log interpolation, and independently check its
accuracy and kernel normalization. Plummer's radial decrease gives the tighter
H<=1/2, so conditional g/g_b<=1+alpha. This tighter bound is not used as the
general-profile diagnostic. Nuclear/stellar binary or host fields are unmodeled.
