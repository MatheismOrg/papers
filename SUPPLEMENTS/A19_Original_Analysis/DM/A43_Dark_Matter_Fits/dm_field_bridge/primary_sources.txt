Primary literature consulted for the physical-source bridge, 29 October 2026

Rahvar, S.; Mashhoon, B. (2014). Observational Tests of Nonlocal Gravity:
Galaxy Rotation Curves and Clusters of Galaxies. Physical Review D89,104011.
DOI:10.1103/PhysRevD.89.104011
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4819
The positive astronomical q0 kernel, alpha mu lambda0=2, and frozen values
alpha=10.94+/-2.56 and mu=.059+/-.028/kpc appear in the primary article.
They were inferred in prior work; this run does not remeasure them.

Moffat, J.W.; Rahvar, S. (2013). The MOG weak field approximation and
observational test of galaxy rotation curves. MNRAS436,1439-1451.
DOI:10.1093/mnras/stt1670
https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6383
Moffat, J.W.; Rahvar, S. (2014). The MOG Weak Field approximation II.
Observational test of Chandra X-ray Clusters. MNRAS441,3724-3732.
DOI:10.1093/mnras/stu855
https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.5077
The latter explicitly states alpha=8.89+/-.34 and mu=.042+/-.004/kpc,
from galaxy fits, then tests that pair on clusters. This bridge tests that
particular constant-parameter weak-field prescription.

Chicone, C.; Mashhoon, B. (2016). Nonlocal Gravity in the Solar System.
Classical and Quantum Gravity33,075005.
DOI:10.1088/0264-9381/33/7/075005
https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.01508
Solar applications retain the additional short-distance kernel parameter;
the astronomical q0 approximation is not a complete Solar prediction.

Haghi, H.; Amiri, V. (2016). Testing modified gravity with dwarf spheroidal
galaxies. MNRAS463,1944-1951.
DOI:10.1093/mnras/stw2140
https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/463/2/1944/2892799
Primary Jeans-profile study of eight classical dwarfs reports fixed-MOG
coefficient difficulties with stellar mass-to-light ratios and no common
pair across the objects under their assumptions.

De Martino, I.; Della Monica, R.; Roshan, M. (2025). Dwarf galaxies in
non-local gravity. Physics of the Dark Universe49,102044.
DOI:10.1016/j.dark.2025.102044
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21986
https://arxiv.org/html/2507.21986v1
Uses the same q0 kernel and Plummer sources for eight classical dwarf
dispersion profiles. Includes individual and joint fits, with stellar M/L
and anisotropy nuisance parameters; reports broad compatibility with earlier
work and some parameter tension. Our fixed-M/L half-mass stress test does
not duplicate or supersede that full-profile analysis.

De Martino, I. (2023). Dynamics of dwarf galaxies in scalar-tensor-vector
gravity. Physical Review D108,044074.
DOI:10.1103/PhysRevD.108.044074
https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15908
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.044074
Another primary full-Jeans dSph test; notes tensions in alpha.

Zhoolideh Haghighi, M.H.; Rahvar, S. (2017). Testing MOG, non-local gravity
and MOND with rotation curves of dwarf galaxies. MNRAS468,4048-4055.
DOI:10.1093/mnras/stx692
https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07851
Rotating LITTLE THINGS dwarfs differ from the pressure-supported dSph sample.
The paper already compared these published coefficient pairs and emphasized
the role of stellar mass-to-light ratios. It is not a first-proposal claim here.
