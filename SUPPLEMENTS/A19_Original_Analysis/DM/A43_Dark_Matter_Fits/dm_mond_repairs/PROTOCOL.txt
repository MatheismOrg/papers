Use exactly the earlier equal-population/equal-group log-acceleration objective,

g_b and r, never population/source/host labels. Fit each declared start independently;

Let a_ref=1e-10 SI, x=log10(g_b/a_ref), u=log10(r/kpc), q=x+u, m=x+2u,
s=10^q, U=10^m. F(g,a)=g/[1-exp(-sqrt(g/a))]. g_c=1e-8 SI is fixed.
S=r^4/(r^4+ell^4); W=1/[1+(g_b/g_c)^2].

Seven families:
1 RAR: F(g_b,10^A a_ref); A in [-3,3], start [.1].
2 simple: g_b nu_1(g_b/(10^A a_ref)); same A bounds/start.
3 standard: g_b nu_2(...); same A bounds/start.
4 nu_n: g_b nu_n(...), nu_n(y)=[(1+sqrt(1+4 y^-n))/2]^(1/n).
  Fit [A,n], bounds [-3,.5] to [3,8], starts [.1,1],[.1,2],[0,4].
  n=1 is simple, n=2 standard. For n<=1 absolute high-g residual need not vanish;
  this is a known interpolation-family benchmark, not an asserted Solar-safe law.
  Published formula: Desmond et al. 2024 MNRAS 530 1781,
  https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/530/2/1781/7641422 ; family reviewed in
  Famaey & McGaugh 2012 https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.3960.
5 mixed_transient: ae/a_ref=C0+[C1 U^-alpha S W+C2 s^beta]/(1+r/R).
  Fit [logC0,logC1,logC2,alpha,beta,logell,logR], same seven parameters and
  bounds/starts as prior mixed_regular; this deliberately moves the inverse-mass
  term inside the existing finite-range cutoff to restore a universal isolated
  point-source far-field normalization V_inf^4=G M a_ref C0.
  Bounds [-3,-5,-5,0,0,-2,0] to [3,3,3,.95,.95,0,4]. Starts
  [-.1,-2,-.2,.6,.7,-2,2.5], [0,-1.5,-.5,.3,.3,-1,1.5],
  [-.4,-3,-.7,.9,.9,-1.5,3.5]. The minimum ell=10 pc remains imposed.
6 mixed_potential_taper: ae/a_ref=C0+S W [C1 U^-alpha+C2 s^beta] s/(s+s0).
  Fit [logC0,logC1,logC2,alpha,beta,logell,logs0], bounds
  [-3,-5,-5,0,0,-2,-8] to [3,3,3,.95,.95,0,2]. Starts
  [-.1,-2,-.2,.6,.7,-2,-4], [0,-1.5,-.5,.3,.3,-1,-2],
  [-.4,-3,-.7,.9,.9,-1.5,-6]. This adds no parameter relative to mixed_regular.
  It makes extra terms vanish as s->0, including empty-source and point-source
  far-field limits, while retaining r^4 central and high-g suppression.
7 two_log_bumps: ae/a_ref=C0+C1 exp[-(q-mu1)^2/(2 sigma1^2)]
                                  +C2 exp[-(q-mu2)^2/(2 sigma2^2)].
  Fit [logC0,logC1,mu1,logsigma1,logC2,mu2,logsigma2]. Bounds
  [-3,-5,-6,-.7,-5,0,-.7] to [3,4,0,.7,4,4,.7]. Starts
  [-.1,1,-3,-.1,.5,1.5,-.1], [0,.5,-4,.3,1,2,.3],
  [-.3,2,-2.5,-.4,.5,1,.2]. Seven coefficients, two smooth potential windows,
  no population switch. Common finite C0 at q->+/-infinity preserves empty source,
  smooth central vanishing force, strong high-g recovery and universal asymptotic
  BTFR. Sharp windows might violate monotonicity or positive point-source inferred
  extra density; explicitly test and report, rather than assume admissibility.
