A43 final regularity repair -- 28 October 2026

This third stage is explicitly adaptive, selected after seeing the first-stage
six-family results and the second-stage high-acceleration/range repairs. Those
earlier fits exposed a central singularity and very large stellar-mass responses.
This document and run.py are frozen before any third-stage numerical fit. Exactly
ONE new family is tested here. Earlier unsuccessful families remain in their
original supplements. No additional family or coefficient-bound search is allowed.

Define a_ref=1e-10 m/s^2, x=log10(gbar/a_ref), u=log10(r/kpc), q=x+u, m=x+2u,
and F(g,a)=g/[1-exp(-sqrt(g/a))]. Radius r and lengths l,R are in kpc. The new
seven-coefficient family, mixed_regular, is

 a_eff/a_ref = 10^A
  + 10^(B-alpha*m) [r^4/(r^4+l^4)]/[1+(gbar/1e-8)^2]
  + 10^(C+beta*q)/[1+r/R],
 g_pred=F(gbar,a_eff).

Fit A,B,C,alpha,beta,log10(l/kpc),log10(R/kpc). Bounds are
 A:[-3,3], B,C:[-5,3], alpha,beta:[0,.95], log10(l/kpc):[-2,0],
 log10(R/kpc):[0,4]. The minimum l=10 pc is imposed before fitting to reduce
 the unphysical inverse-mass term in stellar systems; it is NOT inferred from
 binary data. It does not suppress the base C0 MOND-like term and does not
 establish wide-binary or Cassini compatibility. The gate powers 4 and 1 and
 high-acceleration regulator 1e-8 SI are fixed, not fitted.

Three fixed starts (A,B,C,alpha,beta,logl,logR):
 [-.1,-2.,-.2,.6,.7,-2.,2.5]
 [0.,-1.5,-.5,.3,.3,-1.,1.5]
 [-.4,-3.,-.7,.9,.9,-1.5,3.5]
Every start is tried independently with at most 2000 least-squares evaluations.
Retain all costs, coefficients, convergence/bound diagnostics and any failures.
Choose the lowest objective from the declared starts, recording its success flag.
No per-object, population, host or source offsets occur in the model.

Physical motivation and limits:
The radius^4 gate makes the inverse leg vanish in a smooth central baryonic core,
where gbar~r and m~3log(r): the gated leg~r^(4-3alpha), with exponent>=1.15.
The positive gate has power1, ensuring its fixed-point-mass logarithmic radial
slope is at least-(beta+1)>-2; this prevents a negative inferred point-source
extra density from an excessively rapid radial cutoff. The family retains a
positive 1/r point-mass far field and vanishes with baryons at fixed positive r.
These are empirical coordinate-based repairs. GQUMOND screening is established
motivation, but this formula is not derived from a GQUMOND action or any covariant
field equation. No claim of conserved arbitrary-source dynamics follows.
