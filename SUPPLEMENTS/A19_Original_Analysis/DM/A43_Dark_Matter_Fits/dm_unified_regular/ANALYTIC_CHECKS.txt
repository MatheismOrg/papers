Analytical checks for the one mixed_regular family -- 28 October2026

Set U=gbar*r^2/(a_ref*kpc^2), V=gbar*r/(a_ref*kpc), all positive. Write
 a_eff/a_ref=C0+N+P,
 N=C1*U^(-alpha)*S(r)*W(gbar), S=r^4/(r^4+l^4),
 W=1/[1+(gbar/gc)^2], gc=1e-8m/s^2,
 P=C2*V^beta/(1+r/R).

At fixed positive r, gbar->0 still gives N~gbar^(-alpha), so
g_pred~gbar^((1-alpha)/2)->0 for alpha<=.95. This is a limit statement, not a
claim that a_eff itself vanishes. At fixed r and gbar->infinity, N vanishes
as gbar^(-alpha-2), while P grows no faster than gbar^.95. Thus
sqrt(gbar/a_eff) grows unbounded and the exponential RAR correction vanishes
in both relative and absolute acceleration.

For a smooth constant-density baryonic core, gbar~r and U~r^3, V~r^2.
N~r^(4-3alpha), with exponent>=1.15. P tends to0 if beta>0 and to a finite
constant if beta=0. Hence a_eff has a finite positive central limit, and
g_pred~sqrt(r)->0. This cures the inverse-mass central divergence; it does not
assert differentiability of the MOND-like central field at r=0.

At fixed isolated point-source mass M and r->infinity, U is constant,
S,W->1, and P~r^(-beta-1)->0. Thus
 a_eff,infinity=a_ref*[C0+C1*U^(-alpha)],
 g_pred~sqrt(GM*a_eff,infinity)/r.
Flat asymptotic circular speeds survive, but their normalization is mass-dependent:
 V_infinity^4=G*M*a_ref*[C0+C1*U^(-alpha)].
The low-mass asymptote still gives V_infinity^4~M^(1-alpha), whereas high mass
approaches constant a_ref*C0. The finite-radius gates do not repair a universal
baryonic Tully-Fisher law across every mass.

Point-source extra-density positivity -- analytic proof
For fixed M, the logarithmic radius slopes of the three positive a_eff legs are
 0,
 n_N=4*(1-S)+4*(1-W)>=0,
 n_P=-beta-r/(r+R)>-(beta+1)>=-1.95.
The logarithmic slope n=dlog(a_eff)/dlog(r) is their positive weighted average,
so n>-2. With t=sqrt(GM/(r^2*a_eff)), the RAR gives
 M_extra(r)=M/[exp(t)-1].
Since dlog(t)/dlog(r)=-1-n/2<0, M_extra is increasing. More explicitly,
 dM_extra/dr = (M/r) * t*exp(t)/[exp(t)-1]^2 * (1+n/2) >=0.
Therefore rho_extra=(4*pi*r^2)^(-1)dM_extra/dr is nonnegative at every positive
radius for every allowed parameter set in the point-source calculation. This
explains choosing gate power1 rather than2. It does not prove positive effective
density for arbitrary extended baryonic profiles, conservation, stability, or
consistency with gravitational lensing.

Solar and binary interpretation
The chosen l>=10pc is an imposed screening prior, not a measurement of that scale.
It suppresses the formerly enormous inverse-mass response at stellar separations.
The constant C0 term remains, so low-acceleration wide binaries can still have
ordinary MOND-like enhancements. solar_stress.csv reports isolated predictions
at six separations, including1000 and10000AU; none is a fitted orbital residual.
The Sun in the Galactic field requires a vector field calculation, and Cassini's
external-field quadrupole cannot be inferred from this scalar radial formula.
