Frozen before fitting, 2026-10-29.

Question: Does a published concentration-mass relation improve held-out-galaxy
prediction over shared RAR and purely empirical shared NFW mappings? This is a
restricted cosmological-prior baseline, not a test of the full LCDM framework.

Data: exact copy of dm_shared_halo/input.csv, 2700 SPARC rows, 149 galaxies.
Independent catalog features: Mproxy/Msun=1e9(0.5 L3.6+1.33 MHI), Reff in kpc.
Mproxy is an approximate cold-baryonic mass proxy, not an exact baryonic mass:
one stellar M/L=0.5 for total luminosity, no molecular gas, no uncertainty
propagation. Supplied radial gbar retains source disk/bulge conventions.
No Vflat, observed velocity, or acceleration residual enters any input feature.

Two and only two fitted candidate families:
1 NFW_Planck_mass: Mref=Mproxy/fb+10^A (Mproxy/1e10 Msun)^B Msun.
2 NFW_Planck_size: Mref=Mproxy/fb+10^A (Mproxy/1e10 Msun)^B
  (Reff/3 kpc)^C Msun.
Bounds: A in [8,14], B in [0.1,2], C in [-1.5,1.5].
Starts: mass (11.5,0.7),(12,1.2),(10.5,0.4); size appends C=0,0.5,-0.5.
These are our phenomenological shared mass mappings, not published abundance
matching relations. The floor enforces the approximate proxy budget Mproxy<=
fb*Mref as a model restriction, not a universal observational theorem.

Published halo structural prior: Dutton & Maccio 2014, arXiv:1402.7073,
Eq.8 (NFW, z=0, relaxed dark-matter-only Planck halos):
log10 c200=0.905-0.101 log10[Mref/(1e12 h^-1 Msun)].
Table1 Planck values h=0.671, Omega_m=0.3175, Omega_b=0.0490;
fb=Omega_b/Omega_m. This uses physical solar masses: mass argument Mref*h/1e12.
r200=(G*Mref/(100 H0^2))^(1/3), rs=r200/c200, H0=67.1 km/s/Mpc.
Mref is explicitly the DMO-equivalent reference mass (all matter represented by
collisionless particles), not the present dark-only or total observed M200.
Dark component is (1-fb)*Mref, with the same NFW shape, no halo response.
The observed baryonic field is added separately. The final total distribution
need not have its actual 200critical radius at this reference r200.
f(z)=ln(1+z)-z/(1+z).
gpred(r)=gbar(r)+G*(1-fb)*Mref*f(min[r/rs,c200])/(f(c200)*r^2).
This explicitly truncates the reference halo at r200, ensuring finite total
dark mass; count any observed points beyond that radius. Truncation leaves
interior force unchanged under spherical symmetry.
All fitted halo accelerations are positive. With B>0, at fixed r>0 and Reff,
Mproxy->0 implies Mref->0 and eventually r>r200, so the halo force vanishes.

Fitting and validation (identical shared_halo protocol): equal-galaxy mean
squared log10 acceleration residual. Each galaxy receives equal total weight;
each retained radial point receives equal weight within its galaxy. This is
not a measurement-error likelihood. All-data fits are descriptive only.
Group-fivefold CV seeds: 20261027,7331,48105; RNG default_rng(seed), permute
sorted unique group labels, assign j modulo5. All rows of each galaxy stay
together. Four feature-only tail tests hold out exactly37 galaxies with lowest
or highest Mproxy or Mproxy/(2*pi*Reff^2), sorted by (feature,system name).
Training-only fits for all validation predictions. Each family has20 fit sets:
1 all-data+15 CV+4 tails. All3 deterministic starts run for each; choose minimum
training cost; retain convergence, boundaries, training rows and coefficients.
Scipy least_squares, max_nfev3000, ftol=xtol=gtol=1e-10.

Report all candidates, every seed and every tail. Metrics: equal-galaxy RMS dex,
row RMS dex, row-median absolute fractional acceleration and circular-speed
errors. Speed=the circular-speed-equivalent metric sqrt(gpred/gobs), not an
independent observation. Compare frozen identical-split shared_halo prediction
tables, checking row IDs, folds and numerical input fields. Record source
hashes. Descriptive paired Poisson galaxy bootstrap5000, seed20261029, for RMS
difference versus RAR and shared empirical NFW_mass/NFW_size. No optimizer
refitting, model-search correction, systematic-uncertainty marginalization,
or independent confirmatory interpretation of these bootstrap intervals.

Source conventions checked, not silently mixed:
Moster,Naab,White2013 arXiv:1205.5807 explicitly uses200critical 'virial' masses
(intro and sec2.1), not Bryan-Norman masses in this version. It uses WMAP7
(Omega_m=.272,h=.704), whereas the selected cM relation is Planck. No frozen
Moster SHMR is inserted into this Planck calculation. Its satellite mapping
depends on infall mass/redshift, also absent here. We do not claim to have
tested the published Moster+NFW population model or abundance matching.

Limitations to retain prominently: deterministic median cM and mass mappings,
ignored intrinsic cM/SHMR scatter, no galaxy formation response or feedback,
no external-field/environment/assembly/satellite treatment, no distance,
inclination, M/L or feature measurement-error marginalization, and no lensing,
cluster, CMB or growth likelihood. Published cM fits cover approximately
1e10 to1e15 h^-1 Msun; report extrapolated predictions, do not clip masses.
Poor performance rejects only these restricted deterministic mappings.
