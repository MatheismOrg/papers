A43 constrained shared-response follow-up, 28 October 2026
Fixed before this follow-up's numerical fits, after inspection of prior A43 results
and predictor ranges. Exploratory analysis on a repeatedly inspected benchmark;
this is not preregistration or an untouched discovery test.

Input: frozen dm_broad_joint/combined_input.csv, 2,910 rows, five populations,
279 systems represented by 278 conservative validation groups. No new measured
observable is introduced. x=log10(gbar/a_ref), u=log10(r/kpc), a_ref=1e-10 SI.
q=x+u and m=x+2u. The latter is log10 of the spherical-equivalent baryonic mass
in units a_ref*kpc^2/G. For disks this is not the actual enclosed mass.

All six new families use one common coefficient set, no population label or
object-specific adjustment. Write F(g,a)=g/[1-exp(-sqrt(g/a))]. Define
P(s,t)=10^A+10^(B-alpha*s)+10^(C+beta*t), with alpha,beta in [0,0.95].
1. mass_power: g=F(gbar,a_ref*P(m,m)); 5 coefficients.
2. mass_bounded: g=F(gbar,a_ref*10^(A+B*z+C*z^2)),
   z=tanh((m-m0)/w); 5 coefficients, C>=0, w>0.
3. mass_additive: g=gbar+sqrt(gbar*a_ref*P(m,m)); 5 coefficients.
4. mixed_power: g=F(gbar,a_ref*P(m,q)); 5 coefficients.
5. index_power: g=F(gbar,a_ref*P(m,x+k*u)); 6 coefficients, k in [0,2].
6. mass_screened: g=gbar+sqrt(gbar*a_ref*P(m,m))*
   exp[-sqrt(gbar/(a_ref*10^A))]; 5 coefficients.
The fitted domains, exact starts, and computational bounds are frozen in run.py.
Three fixed starts per new family. No starts derived from test-fold targets.

These are empirical closures, not new covariant theories. The mass-based negative
leg is constant at fixed isolated source mass while r varies. The positive leg
is constant (m), vanishes (q), or stays bounded (x+k*u, 0<=k<=2) as r->infinity.
Thus g~constant(M)/r in that point-mass far field. At fixed positive radius,
g->0 when gbar->0 because alpha<1; g/gbar->1 as gbar->infinity because beta<1
(or because a_eff is bounded / the explicit screening exponential vanishes).
Those limits do not establish Cassini, extended-source consistency, a metric,
conservation, lensing, or cosmological predictions. A mass-dependent coefficient
changes the baryonic Tully-Fisher normalization/slope across masses; quantify it.

Matched refits of existing RAR, potential_quad, power_sum, and half_power are
comparators. Preserve the original equal-population, equal-group, equal-row
within-group objective and fold construction. Run all-data fits; whole-group
five-fold CV at seeds 20261027, 7331, 48105; leave each complete population out;
and exclude every dSph of MW or M31 before predicting that host. Retain all
fold coefficients, raw predictions, optimizer convergence and bound diagnostics.
No dropping failed or poor families.

Primary criterion: improve group and population transfer relative to both the
previous potential quadratic and power sum while meeting the stated limits.
Report each population separately, including SPARC speed errors. A small gain
in one score does not establish dominance or superiority to all published models.
Descriptive paired 5,000-draw group-Poisson bootstrap on fixed primary fold and
population-holdout predictions; it excludes refitting, selection and reconstruction
uncertainty. For host tests, provide each host score without a two-host confidence
claim. Preserve all attempted families and all successful/failed optimization calls.
