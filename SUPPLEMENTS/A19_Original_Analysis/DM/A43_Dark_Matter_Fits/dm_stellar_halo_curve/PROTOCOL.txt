MOTIVATION
Likelihood-fitted NFW with halo variation improves frozen dwarf central-prediction
RMS but worsens disk point RMS. Its mass scaling is shallower, suggesting that
stellar-to-halo curvature should be tested directly. Such curvature is established
prior art, not an original A43 concept (Moster, Naab & White 2013).

Use the same 149 SPARC galaxies, 2700 rows, original 3 x 5 folds and 4 feature
tails. Fit only disk training outcomes, preserving old RAR/NFW/blend comparators.
Same finite NFW halo and published Planck concentration relation. Every rule
returns Mref >= Mp/fb. No concentration offset or fitted per-galaxy state here.
Mstar=0.5 L3.6 as before; Mp=Mstar+1.33 MHI; Re is photometric.

Four families, all retained:
(1) Moster fixed: invert the z=0 median relation Mstar/Mhalo=
2N/[(Mhalo/M1)^-beta+(Mhalo/M1)^gamma], with logM1=11.59, N=.0351,
beta=1.376, gamma=.608 as quoted by Li et al. 2020. Mref=max(M_AM,Mp/fb).
This is an inverse median relation, not p(Mhalo|Mstar) with selection/scatter.
(2) Moster scaled: Mref=Mp/fb+10^eta (Re/3)^C max(M_AM-Mp/fb,0).
Fit eta in [-2,2] and C in [-1.5,1.5]. The exact unscaled endpoint retained.
(3) Stellar power: Mref=Mp/fb+10^A (Mstar/1e10)^B (Re/3)^C.
A [8,14], B [.1,2], C [-1.5,1.5]. Three old-style starts.
(4) Stellar curved: x=log10(Mstar/1e10), z=log10(Re/3),
soft(u)=log10(1+10^(2u))/2,
log10(Mex)=A+B*x+Delta*[soft(x-xt)-soft(-xt)]+C*z.
A [8,14], B [.1,2], Delta [0,2], C [-1.5,1.5], xt [-3,1].
Thus log M is smoothly convex in log stellar mass; low/high slopes B/B+Delta.
Transition Mstar=10^(10+xt), smoothness fixed. Five shared coefficients.
Three declared starts plus the exact Delta=0 stellar-power endpoint and a
near-endpoint start. This is empirical population scaling, not new field physics.

Objective: original equal-galaxy mean squared log acceleration error. SciPy
least_squares max_nfev2000, tol1e-10, retain every attempt, no outcome-selected
new starts. Report all repeated-fold, tail and full-SPARC frozen 44-dwarf results.
Dwarf inputs use total stellar mass and measured projected Re, not dispersion or
an inferred halo mass. They are mass-estimator stresses, not Jeans fits. Dwarfs
and all SPARC observations were previously inspected; no blind discovery claim.

Audit: Moster root identity, exact baseline nesting, monotonic positive mass,
shared fold exclusions, independently recomputed metrics and conditional paired
whole-galaxy intervals. Publish all outcomes even if none beats RAR/blend.
Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.5807 and
https://arxiv.org/html/2001.10538v2; concentration source as in dm_cosmological_halo.

ADAPTIVE ENSEMBLE FOLLOW-UP
After the four declared families were evaluated, stellar_power preserved disk
RMS approximately and improved frozen dwarf transfer. Apply the already-used
RAR/NFW convex force-blend algorithm with stellar_power replacing the former
baryonic-mass NFW base. No changed weight-fitting algorithm: fit one common w on
training predictions of the separately trained bases, freeze for each test set.
Five global estimated coefficients; no inner cross-fitting, no new physical law.
This follow-up is explicitly post-result and retains every partition/tail/dwarf.
