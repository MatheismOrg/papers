Independent audit of the six new A43 shared families -- 28 October 2026

Definitions: s=g_b/a_ref>0, R=r/kpc>0, U=sR^2, V=sR. Powers use positive
quantities. P(U,W)=C0+C1*U^(-alpha)+C2*W^beta with all Cj>0 and
0<=alpha,beta<=0.95. The coordinates m=log10(U), q=log10(V) are correct.
U is proportional to spherical-equivalent baryonic mass, not actual enclosed
mass in a general flattened system. No extra measured predictor is introduced.

Counts: mass_power, mass_bounded, mass_additive, mixed_power, and mass_screened
each have five fitted coefficients. index_power has six, including k in [0,2].
The bounded family has log10(a_eff/a_ref)=A+Bz+Cz^2 with
z=tanh((m-m0)/w), C>=0 and w>=0.2. All parameter bounds are finite.

Fixed-radius limits
For s->0 with R fixed, U~s, W~s in all power families. If alpha>0,
a_eff~s^(-alpha), and the RAR or additive low-g term scales as
g_pred~s^((1-alpha)/2). This vanishes because alpha<1, including the boundary
alpha=0.95 (exponent 0.025, extremely slow). If alpha=0, a_eff tends to a
finite positive constant and the low-g square-root limit holds. The screened
family has exponential multiplier tending to1 at s->0, so the same result holds.
For the bounded family, z->-1 and a_eff stays finite: g_pred~sqrt(g_b*a_eff).

For s->infinity with R fixed, a_eff grows at most as s^beta. Since beta<1,
s/(a_eff/a_ref)->infinity, so the RAR ratio g_pred/g_b->1 and its absolute
excess also tends to0. The bounded family likewise has a finite limiting scale.
mass_additive has ratio excess sqrt(a_eff/g_b)->0, but its absolute extra
acceleration grows as s^((1+beta)/2): relative Newtonian recovery is not zero
absolute anomalous force. mass_screened's explicit exp[-sqrt(s/C0)] suppresses
its absolute correction. These are mathematical limits, not observational tests.

Fixed-source far field
For an isolated point mass M, U=GM/(a_ref*kpc^2) is constant and s=U/R^2.
mass_power, mass_additive, mass_screened and mass_bounded have constant a_eff.
mixed_power has W=V=U/R, so its positive leg vanishes for beta>0, or is a
constant if beta=0. index_power has W=sR^k=UR^(k-2): it vanishes for k<2,
beta>0, and is constant for k=2 or beta=0. Thus every new family has a finite
strictly positive a_eff,lim(M), and g_pred~sqrt(GM*a_eff,lim(M))/r. The screened
exponential tends to1. All claimed 1/r limits are correct, including parameter
boundaries. Finite positive C0 prevents the leading asymptotic amplitude vanishing.

These limits preserve asymptotically flat circular speeds but do not preserve a
universal baryonic Tully-Fisher normalization:
  V_inf^4=G M a_eff,lim(M).
For mass_power/additive/screened, a_eff,lim=a_ref*P(U,U). Its low-M inverse
leg gives V_inf^4 proportional to M^(1-alpha), and high-M positive leg gives
M^(1+beta), when those legs dominate. At alpha=0.95 the former exponent is0.05.
For mixed_power and index_power with k<2,beta>0, the positive leg vanishes in
the ultimate far field; the surviving low-M inverse leg still breaks constant
normalization, while large M approaches C0. index_power at exactly k=2 instead
has mass_power asymptotics. The bounded family has finite limiting normalizations
at both mass extremes but a variable one across the transition.

Additional path: smooth baryonic center
The fixed-radius low-g test does not imply regular behavior at a baryonic core.
For a smooth nonzero central density, g_b is proportional to r while U is
proportional to r^3. The negative-mass leg gives a_eff proportional to
r^(-3alpha), hence g_pred proportional to r^((1-3alpha)/2). For alpha>1/3
this diverges at r->0, although the baryonic density is regular. Every fitted
power family reaches alpha=0.95, producing an exponent of -0.925. The additive
and screened versions share the pathology because their low-g screening tends
to1. mass_bounded avoids it because a_eff has a finite low-mass bound. This is
a mathematical singularity outside the sampled ranges, not an empirical score
error, but it obstructs treating the fitted expression as a globally regular
force law. A high-g-only regulator does not cure it because g_b->0 at the center.
A universal turnover C1*(U+U0)^(-alpha), U0>0, would cure this particular
singularity and needs its own declared fit and transfer tests.

Finite-Solar predictions are materially different from asymptotic recovery
The all-data mixed_power fit evaluated for an isolated one-solar-mass source has
a_eff about 2.03e-5 m/s^2. Its acceleration exceeds Newtonian by about 20.2% at
9.58 AU and 130% at 30 AU. mass_power, mass_bounded and index_power have similar
failures; mass_additive is already about 6% high at 1 AU. mass_screened suppresses
those three illustrated Solar corrections, but has worse population transfer.
The exact illustrative values and declared constants are in solar_stress.csv.
These are not planetary-data fits or a determination of confidence exclusions.
They demonstrate that the attractive analytic limits alone cannot certify a
universal gravity law. Cassini's external-field quadrupole additionally requires
the actual gravitational field equation and Galactic boundary field.

Implementation audit
run.py's logarithmic predictions match dimensionful equations independently
evaluated in verify.py. The least-squares objective has the advertised equal
population/group weighting. Whole groups stay together in each recorded fold.
Population exclusions remove all groups of that population. Host tests exclude
all 26 MW or 18 M31 dSph entries, with 2884 or 2892 remaining rows respectively.
The sole optimizer failure in mass_screened/population_dSph start2 was not the
selected fit; a successful start had lower objective. All attempts are retained.
No gamma functions occur in this implementation; powers and coordinates are
explicit and dimensionless. No field-level or gamma-function approximation is
being smuggled into these regression formulas.
