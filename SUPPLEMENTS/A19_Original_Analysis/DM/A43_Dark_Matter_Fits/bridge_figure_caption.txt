Primary bridge tests use fivefold whole-object exclusion (seed 20261027); panels
refer to different samples and cannot be pooled into one score. Lower is better.
(A) Equal-galaxy RMS log10 acceleration differences relative to the refitted
exponential RAR; bars give descriptive 95% paired galaxy-bootstrap intervals of
fixed validation residuals. Both shared NFW relations overlap zero. The published
c(M) relation is fixed; the three fitted coefficients relate halo mass to observed
baryonic mass and size. (B) Equal-dwarf RMS for 44 half-light acceleration proxies,
assuming isolated spherical Plummer stars and M/L_V=2. Fixed NLG/MOG parameters
come from published galaxy/cluster studies; fixed RAR uses a0=1.2e-10 m/s^2.
Shared dwarf refits are scored on excluded objects. Both kernel refits approach
their inverse-length upper bound; this is not a full Jeans or complete-theory
test. (C,D) Simple-MOND gas extensions on the same selected cold-disk and hot-cluster
rows: no added gas source, one calibration shared across both domains, or separate
domain calibrations. Each host receives equal weight; the cluster score also
balances CLASH and X-COP sources. The disk extension uniformly weights the gas
source; it is not a finite-cutoff disk convolution or the published hot-only
proposal. The shared gas calibration compromises between the two samples.
