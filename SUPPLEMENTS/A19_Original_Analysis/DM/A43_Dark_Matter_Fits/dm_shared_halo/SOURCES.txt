Primary source provenance and relevant prior work
Accessed 29 October 2026. This file contains notes, not copies of source papers.

1. SPARC database and Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert (2016), AJ 152,157,
   "SPARC: Mass Models for 175 Disk Galaxies with Spitzer Photometry and Accurate
   Rotation Curves", DOI 10.3847/0004-6256/152/6/157.
   https://astroweb.case.edu/SPARC/
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251
   Original Table 1 local provenance: ../dm_fit/data/SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt.
   This work uses its independent total 3.6-micron luminosity, effective light
   radius and HI mass, joining to the existing filtered149-galaxy rotation sample.

2. Li, Lelli, McGaugh, Starkman & Schombert (2019), MNRAS482,5106,
   "A constant characteristic volume density of dark matter haloes from SPARC
   rotation curve fits", DOI 10.1093/mnras/sty2968.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00553
   Fits Einasto and DC14 halos to SPARC and relates halo quantities to luminosity.
   Establishes relevant prior work for halo/baryon scaling; the present simple
   NFW/Burkert regression is not the first proposal of shared baryon-halo relations.

3. Navarro et al. (2017), MNRAS471,1841,
   "The origin of the mass discrepancy-acceleration relation in LambdaCDM",
   DOI 10.1093/mnras/stx1705.
   https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/471/2/1841/3939742
   Relates the MDAR to conventional galaxy-formation ingredients including
   baryonic mass, size/radial profile and the galaxy-halo mass relation.
   Its framework is richer than the empirical affine relations tested here.

4. Li et al. (2020), ApJS247,31,
   "A comprehensive catalog of dark matter halo models for SPARC galaxies",
   DOI 10.3847/1538-4365/ab700e.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10538
   Seven halo families with MCMC treatment of galaxy nuisance parameters and
   cosmological relations. These fits are not reproduced by the frozen-nuisance,
   shared four/six-parameter test here. Avoid claiming to outperform that catalog
   or all halo models from the results of this narrow comparison.

The familiar spherical NFW and Burkert density-profile acceleration integrals
are explicitly recorded in PROTOCOL.txt and run.py. Existing exponential RAR and
A43 mixed_regular implementations were preserved from the previous analysis.
The novelty, if any, concerns this particular transparent validation comparison,
not the halo profiles, a relation between baryons and halos, or universal gravity.
