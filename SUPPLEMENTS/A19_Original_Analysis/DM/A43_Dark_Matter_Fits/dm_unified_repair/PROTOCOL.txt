A43 screened response and fixed-radius cluster follow-up, 28 October 2026

This second stage was chosen after inspecting the first constrained-family fits,
their Solar point-mass failures and earlier X-COP stress results. It is explicitly
post-selection exploratory work, not an independent confirmation. This protocol
is fixed before any second-stage fit. Exactly two new families are tested; all
attempts and diagnostics must remain available.

Use a_ref=1e-10 m/s^2, x=log10(gbar/a_ref), u=log10(r/kpc), m=x+2u, q=x+u.
Mass is spherical-equivalent gbar*r^2/G, not actual enclosed disk baryonic mass.
F(g,a)=g/[1-exp(-sqrt(g/a))]. Let gc=1e-8 m/s^2, a fixed regulator scale already
used in earlier A43 tests, NOT fitted here; W=1/[1+(gbar/gc)^2].

(1) mixed_gate, 5 coefficients:
 a_eff/a_ref = 10^A + W*10^(B-alpha*m) + 10^(C+beta*q).
 g=F(gbar,a_eff), alpha,beta in [0,0.95].
(2) mixed_gate_range, 6 coefficients:
 same expression, but divide its positive-potential term by 1+(r/rc)^2.
 log10(rc/kpc) is fitted in [0,4]. This permits a radial turn-down; it is not a
 derived field equation. Test effective-density positivity rather than assuming it.

The inverse-mass gate suppresses the first-stage finite-Solar anomaly. Both
families retain a positive 1/r isolated point-mass far field and vanish as
baryons vanish at fixed positive radius. Neither guarantees conserved extended-
source dynamics, metric lensing, cosmology, the Cassini external-field response,
or a correct baryonic Tully-Fisher relation. Mathematical spot checks are not
observational likelihood fits. Exact starts and bounds are frozen in run.py.
Three fixed starts per new family, capped 1600 evaluations per start.
