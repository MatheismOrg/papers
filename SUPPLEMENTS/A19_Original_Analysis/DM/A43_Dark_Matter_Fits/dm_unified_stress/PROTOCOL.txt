A43 frozen fixed-radius cluster transfer, 28 October 2026

Written before the new candidates' X-COP predictions are calculated. The source
X-COP data and prior analyses have already been inspected. This is exploratory
transfer on previously inspected objects, not untouched survey validation.

For ALL ten shared models in dm_unified_limits/results/results.json, use only
the coefficients fitted to the original five-population, 279-system benchmark.
No coefficient or cutoff is fitted or selected using these X-COP observations.
Apply the frozen response to the 21 archived profiles at independently selected
500, 750 and 1000 kpc radii in seven clusters, using dm_fixed_radius inputs.

Primary case is forward hydrostatic mass and gas plus 0.75 projected stellar
mass. Also report all nine pre-existing combinations of forward/NFW/Einasto
target and zero/0.75/1 projected stellar-mass contribution. These are source
model/systematic sensitivities, not independent observations or posterior draws.

Score equal hosts then equal radii in logarithmic RMS and pooled median absolute
fractional acceleration error. Record all predictions, original input hash and
fit-result hash. No score is a claim of likelihood-based exclusion, universal
fit, or superiority over full alternative gravitational theories.
