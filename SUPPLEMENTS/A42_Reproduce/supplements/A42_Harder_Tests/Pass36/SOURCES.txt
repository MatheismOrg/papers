Primary sources consulted for Pass36, 31 October 2026 UTC

1. Bueno, Cano, Hennigar and Murcia, Regular black hole formation in
four-dimensional non-polynomial gravities, arXiv:2509.19016v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19016v2
Published gravity model; Eq. (44) gives its closed-FLRW Friedmann equation.
Its classical regular solutions are not discoveries of this project.

2. M. J. Duff, Twenty Years of the Weyl Anomaly, hep-th/9308075.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9308075
Euler anomaly coefficients for scalars, Dirac fermions and vectors;
one Weyl fermion contributes half the Dirac value.

3. C. P. Herzog and K.-W. Huang, Stress Tensors from Trace Anomalies
in Conformal Field Theories, Phys. Rev. D 87, 081901 (2013).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.5002
Eq. (23): covariant 4D vacuum stress in a conformally flat background.
Eq. (28): Casimir energy, giving 3 a_Euler/(4L) in four dimensions.
Their prescription removes total-derivative (type D) anomalies.

4. J. E. Lidsey, Thermodynamics of Anomaly-Driven Cosmology,
arXiv:0812.2791.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0812.2791
Derives the anomaly-generated H^4 energy plus an independent radiation
constant in flat FLRW. Here the closed-space generalization is derived
and checked against the covariant tensor, not imported without checking.

5. J. F. Koksma and T. Prokopec, Effect of the Trace Anomaly on the
Cosmological Constant, Phys. Rev. D 78, 023508 (2008).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.4000
Discusses finite curvature couplings, higher derivatives and stability;
lists minimal SM field counts. We use Duff/Lidsey anomaly weights with
an explicit distinction between Dirac and Weyl counts.

Attribution and scope
Quantum trace anomalies, Casimir energy, anomaly-driven cosmology and
the gravity action are established prior work. This package combines
them in a specified test model, derives conditional response/curvature
bounds and supplies reproducible evolution. No claim of first discovery
or of being superior to existing quantum-gravity theories is made.
