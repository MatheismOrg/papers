A42 PASS36: FOUR-DIMENSIONAL CONFORMAL MATTER WITH SELF-CONSISTENT GEOMETRY
Gary Abraham Bernstein research working package -- 31 October 2026 UTC

What is being improved

Pass35 showed why a two-dimensional Polyakov tensor divided by sphere
area cannot simply be promoted to a regular four-dimensional center.
Here the quantum source is replaced in a tractable sector: homogeneous
and isotropic four-dimensional spacetime. It has angular pressure and
agrees with the covariant conformal-vacuum tensor, not merely a radial
ansatz. Its backreaction is solved together with the geometry.

This is a restricted semiclassical construction. It does not repair the
inhomogeneous black-hole calculation or reproduce the interacting SM.
The gravity model and quantum-stress ingredients are published physics;
the contribution here is their explicit combination, bounds and tests.
No publication-priority claim is made.

1. Gravity, matter, state and renormalization

Use the square-root NPQT characteristic function from Bueno et al.:

  h(Phi)=Phi/sqrt(1-alpha^2 Phi^2), alpha>0,
  Phi=H^2+1/a^2, H=dot a/a, D=ddot a/a.

The geometry is closed FLRW with metric signature (-,+,+,+). The
published Friedmann equation is h(Phi)=(8pi G/3)rho_total. The action's
real positive-curvature branch has 0<=alpha Phi<1. alpha is a length
squared, not measured or derived here. The dust density is
rho_d=3 C_d/(8pi G a^3). No cosmological constant is included.

For free massless conformal fields, let

  N_eff=N_s+(11/2)N_W+62N_v,
  a_Euler=N_eff/360, kappa=N_eff hbar/(960pi^2).

N_s counts real conformally coupled scalars, N_W counts two-component
Weyl fermions, N_v counts vectors. These coefficients follow the
standard Euler trace anomaly. Weyl curvature vanishes in FLRW.

SPECIFIED COUPLING CHOICE: the total renormalized coefficient of Box R
in the semiclassical trace equation is set to zero. In a prescribed
background this term can be shifted by a finite R^2 counterterm. Once
gravity is dynamical, its total physical coefficient is an independent
coupling. Setting it to zero restricts the candidate; it is not a proof
that the coefficient is absent in nature. A nonzero coefficient adds
higher derivatives and invalidates the two-variable evolution used here.

The state-dependent homogeneous traceless part is retained as C_q/a^4.
The main calculation sets C_q=0 in the conformal-vacuum prescription
with no type-D (Box R) anomaly. This choice has the expected static
Einstein-universe Casimir energy, rather than incorrectly setting that
vacuum energy to zero. Positive extra radiation/state contributions
are tested separately. Trace information alone never fixes this constant.

2. Full four-dimensional stress in this symmetry sector

The Euler invariant is E4=24 Phi D. The trace and conservation law are

  T_q=-kappa E4/6=-4kappa Phi D,
  dot rho_q+3H(rho_q+p_q)=0.

Using dot Phi=2H(D-Phi), integration gives

  rho_q=kappa Phi^2+C_q/a^4,
  p_q=(kappa/3)(Phi^2-4Phi D)+C_q/(3a^4).             (1)

p_q is all three spatial pressures, including the two angular ones.
It stays finite at the spherical coordinate center when Phi,D,a are
finite. In orthonormal components T=diag(rho_q,p_q,p_q,p_q); there is
no 1/r^2 promotion from a two-dimensional tensor. Coordinate angular
components vanish as p_q r^2 near r=0, as regularity requires.

An independent check uses Herzog and Huang, arXiv:1301.5002, Eq. (23):

  T^mu_nu=-a_Euler hbar/(16pi^2) [
     delta^mu_nu(R^2/2-R_ab R^ab)
     +2R^mu_lambda R^lambda_nu -(4/3)R R^mu_nu ].     (2)

On FLRW the mixed Ricci eigenvalues are (3D,D+2Phi,D+2Phi,D+2Phi).
Substitution reproduces every vacuum component of (1). The code tests
this independently over a grid of Phi,D values. Extra C_q/a^4 is the
separately conserved state/radiation tensor, not part of (2).

Minkowski space has zero vacuum stress. In de Sitter, D=Phi=H_dS^2,
so rho_q=kappa H_dS^4 and p_q=-rho_q. These are external-geometry
checks, not a claim of a source-free de Sitter solution of the combined
gravity equations. On a static three-sphere of radius L, (1) gives
energy 2pi^2 L^3 rho_q=3a_Euler hbar/(4L), agreeing with the Casimir
energy in the same zero-Box-R prescription.

3. One combined background equation

Define eta=(8pi G/3)kappa=N_eff hbar G/(360pi), and let C_r include
the state term plus any independently specified classical radiation.
The self-consistent constraint becomes

  Phi/sqrt(1-alpha^2 Phi^2)-eta Phi^2
                         =C_d/a^3+C_r/a^4.           (3)

This is the improved formula for this sector. The geometry on both sides
changes during evolution; the quantum correction is not evaluated on
a fixed classical orbit. In particular its pressure contains D and
therefore changes the acceleration equation.

For dimensionless x=a/sqrt(alpha), s=t/sqrt(alpha), y=alpha Phi,
lambda=eta/alpha, c=C_d/sqrt(alpha), d=C_r/alpha, define

  F_lambda(y)=y/sqrt(1-y^2)-lambda y^2,
  F_lambda(y)=c/x^3+d/x^4, y=(1+v^2)/x^2, v=dx/ds.  (4)

The local homogeneous time evolution is

  x'=v,
  v'=x [ y-(3c/x^3+4d/x^4)/(2 F_lambda'(y)) ],
  F_lambda'(y)=(1-y^2)^(-3/2)-2lambda y.              (5)

The right side is regular at v=0; a bounce is produced by the equations,
not imposed by changing the sign of velocity. Differentiation shows
that the constraint residual F_lambda(y)-c/x^3-d/x^4 is constant under
(5), hence exact initial satisfaction is propagated in the continuum.
The full angular equation h'(Phi)(D-Phi)=-4pi G(rho_total+p_total)
is also checked directly, including dust and all quantum pressures.

4. Exact algebraic-response condition

For lambda>=0, F_lambda'(y)>0 throughout 0<=y<1 precisely when

  lambda < lambda_star = 8/(3sqrt(3)) = 1.53960071784. (6)

Proof: positivity requires lambda<1/[2y(1-y^2)^(3/2)]. The denominator
has its unique maximum at y=1/2. Below (6), F rises monotonically from
zero to infinity; there is a unique y for every nonnegative total
classical/state density. This is an algebraic background condition,
not a theorem about inhomogeneous characteristics or stability.

For lambda=1.6, a contracting dust example with c=2 reaches F'=0 at
y=0.41483100405, x=2.22907835349, v=-1.03014963551. The proper time
from y=y_fold/2 is finite (0.29060380649 in scaled units). The numerator
in (5) remains positive, so D diverges there. The saved approach data
show increasing curvature as the fold is approached. This is a genuine
failure of this effective equation, not a floating-point zero. It does
not imply that a more complete quantum-gravity theory is singular.

5. Uniform curvature control, including a proved bound for the proxy

For d=0 and lambda<lambda_star, put q=1-lambda/lambda_star>0,
b=3/(2q). Since F'>=q(1-y^2)^(-3/2) and 0<=F<=y/sqrt(1-y^2),
the dimensionless acceleration z=alpha D obeys

  y(1-b+b y^2) <= z = y-3F/(2F') <= y <=1.

The minimum of the cubic lower bound is

  z >= -m,  m=2(b-1)^(3/2)/(3sqrt(3b)).

Consequently the four-dimensional Kretschmann invariant satisfies

  K=12(Phi^2+D^2) <=12[1+max(1,m^2)]/alpha^2.       (7)

This bound is uniform in the dust constant and is analytic, not a grid
observation. Positive radiation can be covered by replacing b=3/(2q)
by b=2/q, since 3rho_d+4rho_r<=4(rho_d+rho_r).

For the specified free SM-count proxy, q=0.42828342147 and
m=0.81412509890<1. Thus K<=24/alpha^2 for the zero-extra-radiation
dust sector. Also rho_q<=kappa/alpha^2 and
|p_q|<=5kappa/(3alpha^2), establishing finite central quantum stress
in this homogeneous sector. A separate numerical minimization gives
1.27458516440 as a sufficient lambda bound from the exact z>=-1
inequality. That optimized number is a numerical result; the simpler
analytic proof above suffices for the SM-count proxy.

Do not extend the classical ceiling to every subcritical lambda:
at lambda=1.5 one tested dust case reaches K alpha^2=618.23, although
it remains finite. At lambda=1.6 the fold can make curvature diverge.
Both behaviors are retained in results.json.

6. Standard Model field-count benchmark and actual runs

Counting only the minimal SM fields before mass generation gives
N_s=4, N_W=45, N_v=12. Using the standard free-field anomaly weights,
N_eff=995.5. With the illustrative normalization alpha=hbar G,
lambda=0.88021525471, below (6). The uniform response condition would
read alpha>0.57171657853 hbar G for this proxy. This is a conditional
model inequality, not a measurement or prediction of a fundamental area.

The proxy omits masses, interactions, running-coupling trace terms and
nonconformal Higgs coupling. It is NOT the full Standard Model. Scalar
conformal coupling is explicitly assumed. Planck-curvature runs are
formal semiclassical tests, not a controlled quantum-gravity expansion.

The main scan uses five lambda choices and three dust constants relative
to each model's turning threshold. Three radiation choices and a fixed
dust comparison were added. In the fixed-dust comparison c=3.22370979547,
the classical minimum scale factor is 1.02712279278; quantum feedback
changes it to 1.01605252375. Both are in units of sqrt(alpha).
The quantum term reaches about 44% of the total energy density along
the proxy trajectories; it is not assumed to be infinitesimal. The
general d=0 fraction is lambda y sqrt(1-y^2)<=lambda/2.

The main proxy bounce radii at 1.1,2,10 times its turning threshold are
1.13917815428, 1.04142662410 and 1.00247688043. The scripts compute
them independently from the turning constraint and from time evolution.
The quantum stress satisfies the anomaly, conservation and angular
equation throughout the saved trajectories. All numerical values and
error measures are regenerated, not printed as preset results.

7. Discrete implementation and limits

model.py supplies one implicit-midpoint update for (5). It evolves
through contraction and expansion without a prescribed bounce. Its
second-order convergence is ordinary numerical-method behavior, not a
new physical discovery or proof of spacetime discreteness. The finite
step has small constraint drift; no exact finite-step conservation or
fundamental code-length claim is made. An implicit root solver and
floating-point arithmetic remain explicit dependencies.

The present improvement is a coherent four-dimensional quantum-matter
benchmark coupled to the same published gravity family, together with
a conditional regularity proof and an explicit excessive-coupling
failure. The next priority is to restore and test the independent R^2
coupling, and to examine inhomogeneous perturbations with a correctly
specified quantum state. Those could still reject the construction.
No black-hole evaporation, information recovery, full SM fit or ToE has
been established. The main A42 manuscript has not been overwritten.
