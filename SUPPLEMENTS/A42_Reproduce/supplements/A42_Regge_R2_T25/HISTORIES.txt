histories.npz holds, for every chain (audit stage A and B, preparations, scan, pilot), the per-sweep columns R, sumV, sumDA and sumR2 (when a > 0) as float32, and the number of equilibration sweeps (thermal), as keys "<set>/<run>/<column>"; sweep i is an equilibration sweep when i < thermal. The full 28-column .dat histories regenerate byte for byte from the seeds in plan.json, plan_B.json and plan_B2.json (scan seeds in scan/jobs.txt and scan4/jobs.txt) with the included engine and the same compiler (gcc -O2).
