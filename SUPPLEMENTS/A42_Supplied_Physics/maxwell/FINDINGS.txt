GRAVITY ON A CLASSICAL ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVE

This is an additional field-type consistency test using exactly the massive
circuit implementation with m=0, no gauge phase and no retuned coefficients.
It is a 3+1 metric with a one-dimensional transverse-wave reduction:
    ds^2=-N(x)^2 dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2, N=1+.25cos(x).
The Eulerian fields are E=(0,E_y(t,x),0), B=(0,0,B_z(t,x)).

Derivation directly from the supplied covariant Maxwell equations:
sqrt(-g)=N, E_y=F_y0/N and F_xy=B_z. The y component of
partial_mu[sqrt(-g) F^(mu nu)]=0 gives
    partial_t E_y = -partial_x(N B_z).
The (0,x,y) Bianchi identity gives
    partial_t B_z = -partial_x(N E_y).
The spatial divergence constraints vanish identically in this ansatz.

Set chi_plus/minus=sqrt(N)(E_y +/- B_z). Then
    partial_t chi_plus = -(N partial_x+N'/2)chi_plus,
    partial_t chi_minus = +(N partial_x+N'/2)chi_minus.
These are exactly the two massless chiral transport operators already tested.
This mapping is special to the stated static plane geometry and transverse
sector. It is not an identification of general spin-1 and spin-1/2 theories.

The conserved Killing energy per unit transverse area is
    K=integral N(E_y^2+B_z^2)/2 dx
     =integral(chi_plus^2+chi_minus^2)/4 dx.
Its coordinate energy flux is N^2 E_y B_z. The local field energy E^2+B^2
is not itself the conserved coordinate-time Hamiltonian density when N varies.

The benchmark evolves two counterpropagating real finite-Fourier packets in
the optical coordinate y=integral dx/N, reconstructs E_y,B_z and compares
with the exact continuum solution. Both pulses are present simultaneously;
no final projection or coefficient fit is applied. Normalization fixes K=.25.
At 1024 sites relative field error is .000282813661; at 2048 it is
.0000706043309. Field errors decrease approximately fourfold per doubling.
Killing-energy drift stays below1e-15 and imaginary field parts are zero.

Conclusion: the same local transport rule passes this GR electromagnetic-wave
sector as well as the previous massless quantum propagation sector. The tests
do not yet cover general 3D Maxwell data, sources, polarization transport in
arbitrary curvature, quantized photons, or source backreaction.

Primary context: S. S. Komissarov, Electrodynamics of black hole
magnetospheres, MNRAS350(2004)427:
https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/350/2/427/1113688
and 3+1 magnetodynamics, MNRAS418(2011)L94:
https://academic.oup.com/mnrasl/article/418/1/L94/1023153
