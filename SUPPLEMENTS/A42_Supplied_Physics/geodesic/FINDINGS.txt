MASSIVE QUANTUM PACKETS AND TIMELIKE GR RAYS

The supplied static metric is ds^2=-N(x)^2 dt^2+dx^2 with
N=1+0.25 cos(x). The Dirac Hamiltonian is
H=-i sigma_z[N partial_x+N'/2]+mN sigma_x.

The positive-band classical Hamiltonian is E=N sqrt(p^2+m^2).
Writing eta=asinh(p/m) gives
    dx/dt=N tanh(eta),  deta/dt=-N',  E/m=N cosh(eta).
The mass cancels for matched initial local velocity. At rest, xddot=-NN'.
Our ray starts at x=pi/2 with eta=0. At t=1 its coordinate is
1.6925655602163747; E/m is conserved to approximately 5.4e-14.

A finite-width quantum packet is not a point trajectory. We therefore use
a controlled narrowing family with masses 8,16,32,64 and initial Gaussian
density width parameter 0.8/sqrt(m), centered at pi/2, zero mean momentum.
The initial spinor is projected onto the local constant-coefficient positive
Dirac band. No output or trajectory is fitted. Both decreasing relative
momentum spread and decreasing spatial width matter in this limit; changing
the mass alone for arbitrary fixed states need not give identical centers.

Independent reference evolution uses the Hermitian Fourier pseudospectral
operator (NP+PN)sigma_z/2 +mN sigma_x, evaluated with expm_multiply.
For m=64, 512 versus 2048 spectral sites agree to 4.86e-15 in full state norm.
This verifies discretization consistency, not a formal uniform continuum
error bound. The packet probability near the periodic seam is negligible.

Quantum reference centers at t=1:
    m=8    1.6779862370    error from point ray 0.0145793232
    m=16   1.6848950782    error from point ray 0.0076704820
    m=32   1.6886231987    error from point ray 0.0039423616
    m=64   1.6905657019    error from point ray 0.0019998583
This is the expected approach to the point-particle GR path as the quantum
family becomes localized and semiclassical, not exact trajectory equality.

The actual local discrete circuit is the SAME implementation used in the
massive packet benchmark, with only its initial state supplied. Every
microstep has calibrated bond gates sandwiched between half mass coins;
even-first and odd-first steps alternate. There is no projection during
the evolution. Full-state errors against the spectral reference fall
approximately fourfold per grid doubling for every tested mass.

At 8192 sites the actual circuit centers are:
    m=8    1.6779847789
    m=16   1.6848889779
    m=32   1.6885987960
    m=64   1.6904678447
For m=64, numerical center error relative to the quantum reference is
9.79e-5, distinct from the physical finite-width difference of about 0.0020
between that quantum reference and the point ray. The corresponding full
state norm error is 0.000848279. Fourier resampling of the independently
converged 2048-site reference supplies the finer comparison grid.

Negative control: use a spatially constant m sigma_x instead of mN sigma_x,
while preserving the same massless propagation speed. At 2048 sites and m=64
the circuit center is 1.5700689945, close to its starting position1.5707963268
and far from the correct ray1.6925655602. The independent wrong-coupling
quantum reference is1.5700501647. A common light cone alone is insufficient.
