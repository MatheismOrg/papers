# Glossary of Terms

**A priori.** Known before experience, from reasoning alone. The PRD is a priori: you do not need to observe the universe to know that everything is patterned or random. Contrast with a posteriori, known from observation.

**Algorithmic incompressibility.** A sequence that cannot be described by anything shorter than itself. This is the formal definition of randomness: no pattern, no shortcut, no compression. A random string of 1,000 digits requires all 1,000 digits to specify. The digits of pi require only a short formula.

**Arrow of computation.** The structural fact that nearly all computations are many-to-one: 1+1=2, but 2=x+y has infinitely many solutions. The inverse does not exist. This is why time has a direction, why complexity grows, and why consciousness runs forward. The thermodynamic arrow (entropy increases) is a consequence. The computational arrow is the foundation.

**Block universe.** The view from general relativity that past, present, and future all exist equally, like locations on a map. Your fifth birthday is as real as this sentence. Time is a dimension, not a flow. Death is a boundary, not an ending.

**Boltzmann brain.** A hypothetical conscious brain that assembles by random chance in an empty universe, hallucinating an ordered world before dissolving. Whether Boltzmann brains dominate over structured observers is an open problem in cosmological measure theory. The generating theory is equally simple for both; algorithmic probability ranks theories, not configurations within them. Any cutoff measure imposed to suppress Boltzmann brain dominance is itself a specification with a K cost.

**Born rule.** The rule that quantum probabilities follow the square of the wave amplitude. If a quantum state has amplitude 1/√3 for an outcome, the probability is 1/3. Why squared? This remains an open question, though several derivations within Many-Worlds have been proposed.

**Chinese Room.** John Searle's thought experiment: a person in a room follows rules to manipulate Chinese symbols without understanding Chinese. Searle argued this shows computation alone cannot produce understanding. Under matheism, the room IS understanding at the system level, just as no single neuron understands what the brain knows.

**Collapse (quantum).** The idea that when you measure a quantum system, its wave of possibilities randomly selects one outcome and discards the rest. Physically undefined: no equation specifies when or how collapse occurs. Each measurement adds a postulate. Many-Worlds avoids this by keeping all outcomes.

**Compactification.** Curling extra dimensions up so small they are undetectable. String theory requires 6 or 7 extra dimensions beyond the 4 we observe, each compactified into a tiny geometric shape. The shape determines particle physics, but specifying it costs thousands of bits.

**Compatibilism.** The position that free will and determinism coexist. Your deliberation is part of the causal chain, determined by your nature, yet yours because your nature is doing the choosing. Distinct from libertarian free will, which requires uncaused causation.

**Conservation law.** A quantity that physics preserves over time. Energy, momentum, angular momentum, and electric charge are conserved. Emmy Noether proved in 1918 that every conservation law corresponds to a symmetry: energy conservation comes from time symmetry, momentum from spatial symmetry.

**Constructor theory.** David Deutsch and Chiara Marletto's framework that reformulates physics in terms of which transformations are possible and which are impossible, rather than what happens. A constructor is a system that performs a task and returns to its ready state. The reset is many-to-one, which contains the arrow of time.

**Dark energy.** The unknown cause of the accelerating expansion of the universe. Space itself is stretching, and the stretching is speeding up. Approximately 68% of the universe's total energy content is dark energy. Its nature remains one of the honest open questions. Simplicity dominance predicts the answer will be simple when found.

**Dark matter.** Matter that interacts gravitationally but not electromagnetically: invisible, but its effects on galaxy rotation and cosmic structure are measured. Approximately 27% of the universe's total energy content. Its identity remains unknown. Simplicity dominance predicts the answer will be simple when found.

**Decoherence.** The process by which a quantum system's possible outcomes become independent of each other through interaction with the environment. Trillions of environmental interactions drown out quantum interference, leaving each outcome isolated in its own branch. This is why we observe definite results rather than quantum superpositions.

**Description-space.** The space of all possible descriptions (specifications) of mathematical structures. A short description occupies a larger fraction of this space than a long one: each additional bit halves the fraction. This is why simpler structures have higher measure.

**Dichotomy.** A division into exactly two categories that exhaust all possibilities. The pattern-randomness dichotomy (PRD) divides everything into patterned or random. Both are mathematical. There is no third option.

**Dualism.** Any philosophy that posits two entirely different kinds of stuff: mind and matter, sacred and profane, natural and supernatural. Under matheism, all dualisms collapse. If two things interact, they must share structure. If they share structure, they are not two kinds of things.

**Emergence.** When a system displays properties that its individual parts do not have. No single water molecule is wet. Wetness emerges from billions interacting. Under matheism, emergence is coarse-graining: many micro-level details map to one macro-level description. The emergent property is real but not additional.

**Entanglement.** A quantum connection between two particles such that measuring one instantly determines the state of the other, regardless of distance. Einstein called it "spooky action at a distance." Under superdeterminism, nothing changes at a distance: the outcomes were determined from the start by shared initial conditions. Under Many-Worlds, both outcomes occur in every branch, and the branches correlate.

**Entropy.** The physicist's measure of disorder. A shuffled deck has high entropy; a sorted deck has low. Under matheism, low entropy is low descriptive complexity. The universe started orderly (low entropy) because low complexity is exponentially favored by algorithmic probability.

**Epistemic.** Relating to knowledge and what we can know, as opposed to ontic (relating to what exists). "We don't know which branch we're on" is epistemic. "All branches exist" is ontic. Under matheism, the ontic question is settled: all consistent structures exist. Most remaining questions are epistemic.

**Eschatological.** Relating to end-times prophecy: beliefs about apocalypse, final judgment, the end of the world. Relevant to game theory because an agent optimizing for apocalypse cannot be deterred by threats to its survival.

**Fine-tuning.** The observation that the physical constants of our universe appear calibrated for complex life. Change gravity slightly, stars cannot form. Adjust the strong force, atoms fall apart. Under matheism, fine-tuning dissolves: every set of constants exists as a mathematical structure. We observe the ones compatible with observers because we could not observe anything else.

**Gauge symmetry.** The principle that physics stays the same when you shift your measuring stick in certain ways. Electromagnetism is a gauge symmetry: shift the voltage everywhere by the same amount and nothing changes. The strong force is a color symmetry: relabel quark colors and nothing changes. Every force reduces to a symmetry.

**Gödel's incompleteness.** Kurt Gödel proved that any formal system powerful enough to express arithmetic contains true statements it cannot prove from within. Like a camera that photographs every corner of a room except the lens you are looking through. The lens is still in the room. Gödel found that mathematics always has more truth than any single system can capture.

**General relativity.** Einstein's theory that gravity is not a force but the curvature of spacetime. A massive object bends space the way a bowling ball bends a mattress. Confirmed to fourteen decimal places. Also shows that space and time are one fabric, and that past, present, and future coexist (the block universe).

**Generating rule.** The shortest description that produces a mathematical structure. The generating rule for the digits of pi is a short formula. The generating rule for a truly random string is the string itself. Under algorithmic probability, structures with shorter generating rules have exponentially higher measure. The generating rule is the structure's identity card.

**Geometric harmony.** The framework developed in A19 where the Standard Model's particle spectrum arises as geometric overtones above the metric bundle, the way musical harmonics arise above a fundamental frequency. String theory heard the right music and guessed the wrong instrument. The strings are geometric overtones: derived, not postulated.

**Heuristic.** A rule of thumb: useful and often right, but not proven. Before algorithmic probability, Occam's Razor was a heuristic. After algorithmic probability, it is a theorem.

**Holographic principle.** The discovery that a black hole's three-dimensional interior maps entirely onto its two-dimensional surface. More generally: any number of dimensions maps to one. Dimensions are how you look at the math, not what the math is.

**Hypergraph.** A generalization of a graph where edges can connect any number of points, not just two. Wolfram's Physics Project proposes that spacetime emerges from the repeated application of simple hypergraph rewriting rules. These rules have very low descriptive complexity, making them exponentially favored by algorithmic probability.

**Indexical.** Relating to a particular point of view: this I, this here, this now. There is only one indexical self because there is only one I-am-here-now at any moment. Under matheism, all conscious patterns share one generic indexical position.

**Instantiation.** The realization of an abstract pattern in a concrete structure. Under matheism, the distinction dissolves: abstract IS concrete. A mathematical structure does not need to be instantiated in some other medium. It exists as itself. The math is the territory.

**Kolmogorov complexity.** The length of the shortest description that generates something. The digits of pi have low Kolmogorov complexity despite being infinitely long, because a short formula generates all of them. A truly random string has high Kolmogorov complexity: no shortcut exists.

**Libertarian free will.** The claim that choices are both uncaused and willed. "Free" means uncaused (randomness, no will). "Will" means determined by a nature (determinism). The conjunction is a square circle.

**Many-to-one.** A function where multiple inputs produce the same output. 3+2=5 and 4+1=5: two inputs, one output. This is the structural root of the arrow of time, the Principle of Least Action, and coarse-graining. Irreversibility is not a physical accident. It is a mathematical fact about functions.

**Many-Worlds interpretation.** The interpretation of quantum mechanics where every possible outcome of every measurement actually occurs, and the universe branches. The wavefunction never collapses. All outcomes coexist. It adds nothing to the Schrödinger equation, making it the simplest interpretation under algorithmic probability.

**Matheism.** Mathematical monism or math pantheism: the position that all reality is identical to mathematical structure, derived from the pattern-randomness dichotomy. Not a hypothesis but a derivation. The math is the territory.

**Mathematical monism.** The position that all reality is one kind of thing: mathematical structure. Also called matheism. Derived from the pattern-randomness dichotomy: everything is patterned or random, both are mathematical, there is no third option.

**Measure.** The probability weight assigned to a mathematical structure in the space of all structures. Think of it as how much room a structure occupies in the space of all possible descriptions. A short description occupies a large fraction. A long one occupies an exponentially smaller fraction. Under algorithmic probability, simpler structures have exponentially higher measure.

**Measure-favored / measure zero.** A structure is measure-favored if it has high probability in description-space (short description, high measure). Measure zero means probability effectively zero: a structure so complex that its description occupies a vanishing fraction of description-space. String theory is effectively measure zero relative to simpler alternatives.

**Metric bundle.** The space of all possible metrics (ways to measure distance) on a 4D spacetime. A metric on 4D spacetime has 10 independent components. To specify which metric you have, you need 4 coordinates plus 10 components: 4+10=14 dimensions. This is the mathematical origin of Weinstein's 14D Geometric Unity. The metric bundle exists the moment spacetime has a way to measure distance. Zero additional specification.

**Microtubules.** Tiny structural tubes inside cells. Roger Penrose proposed that quantum coherence in microtubules gives rise to consciousness. Under matheism, this is one more mathematical explanation of consciousness: structure all the way down.

**Multiverse.** The set of all existing universes. Under matheism, all consistent mathematical structures exist. The multiverse is not a hypothesis but a logical consequence: any filter selecting some structures over others is itself a structure, so all filters exist, including the null filter.

**Noether's theorem.** Emmy Noether proved in 1918 that every continuous symmetry in physics corresponds to a conservation law. Time symmetry gives conservation of energy. Spatial symmetry gives conservation of momentum. Without symmetry, no conservation. Without conservation, no stable structures. Without stable structures, no observers.

**Observer-moment.** A single conscious experience at a specific time and place. Under algorithmic probability, each observer-moment is a prefix in description-space. Earlier observer-moments have shorter prefixes and higher individual measure, but later ones are more numerous. We find ourselves where these two pressures meet.

**Occam's Razor.** The principle that simpler explanations should be preferred. Traditionally a heuristic. Under algorithmic probability, it is a theorem: simpler structures have exponentially higher measure. Every unnecessary bit of specification halves the probability.

**Oikophobia.** Hatred of one's own home, culture, and civilization. The opposite of xenophobia: contempt directed inward rather than outward. Under the consistency test, oikophobia aligns with civilizations whose values contradict one's own stated principles while condemning the civilization that produced those principles.

**Panpsychism.** The view that all matter feels or has experience. Under matheism, the question is structural: does a system model its environment? Self-model? The threshold for feeling is open. The logical error of panpsychism is claiming consciousness precedes math rather than being structure.

**Parsimony.** Economy of explanation. Using fewer assumptions, fewer postulates, fewer bits of specification. Under algorithmic probability, parsimony is not a preference but a mathematical selection effect.

**Past Hypothesis.** The unsolved puzzle of why the universe started in a state of extremely low entropy (high order). Under matheism, it is a theorem, not a postulate: low entropy is low descriptive complexity, and low complexity is exponentially favored by algorithmic probability.

**Path integral.** Richard Feynman's formulation of quantum mechanics where a particle takes every possible path simultaneously. Paths near the classical trajectory reinforce each other; paths far from it cancel. What survives is the classical path. The Principle of Least Action is what remains when everything else cancels out.

**Pattern-randomness dichotomy (PRD).** The logical exhaustion at the foundation of matheism. Everything is either patterned or random. Both are mathematical. There is no third option. This is the proof that all reality can only be mathematical structure.

**Performative contradiction.** Contradicting yourself by the act of making the argument. Arguing that reality is not mathematical structure requires structured argument: concepts in logical relation, premises supporting conclusion. The critic deploys mathematical structure to deny mathematical structure.

**Philosophical zombie.** A hypothetical being physically identical to you but lacking conscious experience. Under matheism, zombies can be labeled but not constructed: if a structure were exactly your math, it would feel as you. The depth of the label is limited by contradiction.

**Planck scale.** The smallest meaningful length in physics, approximately 10⁻³⁵ meters. To inspect anything smaller, you would need to concentrate so much energy in so small a region that it would collapse into a black hole, destroying the target. The act of looking destroys what you are looking at. Below this scale, the concepts of space and distance may break down.

**Poststructuralism.** The philosophical school that showed all signs refer only to other signs in circles, with no ground outside language. It was right that signs are circular. It missed that the circular structure is the math of objective reality.

**PRD (pattern-randomness dichotomy).** The logical exhaustion: everything is either patterned or random. Both are mathematical. There is no third option. This is the proof that all reality can only be mathematical structure.

**Principle of sufficient reason.** The philosophical principle that everything must have a reason for being the way it is. Under matheism, it is satisfied without infinite regress: mathematical structure cannot fail to exist, and the measure over structures is determined by descriptive complexity. The buck stops at mathematical necessity.

**Qualia.** The felt qualities of conscious experience: what red looks like, what pain feels like. Under matheism, qualia are not non-structural extras. They are what certain mathematical structures feel like from inside.

**Quantum mechanics.** The physics of the very small. Particles are not little balls but probability waves, existing as clouds of possibility until measured. The Schrödinger equation describes how these possibilities evolve over time. It is the most precisely tested framework in science.

**Rawls / Veil of ignorance.** John Rawls asked what world you would build if you did not know who you would be in it. Under matheism, this is not a thought experiment but a discovery: you do not know. You are all of them.

**Schrödinger equation.** The single equation that describes how all quantum possibilities evolve over time. It is the core of quantum mechanics. Many-Worlds adds nothing to it. Collapse adds a postulate at every measurement.

**Algorithmic probability.** Simpler mathematical structures have exponentially higher measure: each additional bit of specification halves a structure's probability. Physics fits on one page because compact descriptions have exponentially higher measure. Occam's Razor as theorem.

**Self-modeling.** A system whose internal model includes a representation of itself. Under matheism, consciousness is the felt transitions of a self-modeling mind. A thermostat models temperature. A dog models its environment. A human models itself modeling its environment. Self-modeling is the threshold for consciousness, though where exactly that threshold falls remains open.

**algorithmic probability (Solomonoff, 1964; Schmidhuber, 2000).** The principle that simpler mathematical structures have exponentially higher measure. Each additional bit of specification halves a structure's probability. Physics fits on one page because compact descriptions have exponentially higher measure. Occam's Razor as theorem.

**Solomonoff induction.** Ray Solomonoff's 1964 framework for predicting the future by weighting every possible explanation by how short its description is. Shorter explanations get exponentially higher weight. It is mathematically proven to converge on truth faster than any other method. It is the foundation of algorithmic probability.

**Standard Model.** The theory describing every known particle and force except gravity. The entire recipe fits on a couple of pages: about 25 numbers. Change any slightly and atoms fall apart. It is the most compact successful theory in physics.

**Structure-space.** The space of all possible mathematical structures, each weighted by its algorithmic probability. Algorithmic probability operates over structure-space: it selects which universes have higher measure, not which laws emerge within a universe. This is the arena in which Occam's Razor becomes a theorem.

**Superdeterminism.** The interpretation of quantum mechanics where everything, including measurement choices, was determined from the start by initial conditions. The fine-tuning objection (that initial conditions must be implausibly specific) is a category error: specific is not the same as complex. The digits of pi are infinitely specific yet generated by a short formula.

**Superposition.** A quantum system existing in multiple states at once until something forces a definite outcome. An electron can be spin-up AND spin-down simultaneously. This is not uncertainty about which it is. It is both, the way a guitar string vibrates at multiple frequencies at once. Measurement or environmental interaction (decoherence) resolves the superposition into a definite result. Under Many-Worlds, all results occur in different branches.

**Supersessionism.** The theology that a new covenant replaces and supersedes an old one: Christianity replacing Judaism, Islam replacing both. Under the consistency test, supersessionism places group identity above structural truth.

**Thermodynamic.** Relating to heat, energy flow, and the statistical behavior of large numbers of particles. The thermodynamic arrow of time points from low entropy to high entropy. Under matheism, this is a consequence of computation being many-to-one.

**Transcendental constraint.** A condition that must hold for observation to be possible at all. Not a probabilistic filter but a logical one. Observers require pattern. Pattern is mathematical structure. The question "why do we observe patterns?" is self-answering: observation IS structure recognizing itself.

**Wavefunction.** The master equation describing all quantum possibilities at once. In Many-Worlds, it never collapses: all branches coexist. The total wavefunction is reversible. The functions within each branch are not. Both are mathematical structure.

**Zoroastrianism.** The ancient Persian religion founded on the cosmic struggle between light (Ahura Mazda) and darkness (Angra Mainyu). Christianity imported its dualism of God and Devil from this source, then blamed Jews for the Satan it invented.
